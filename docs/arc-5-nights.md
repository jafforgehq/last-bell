# Five nights: the arc (for the owner to approve)

Written 2026-10-10 (step 2 of `docs/plan-7-day-demo.md`). A proposal: nothing is built and `end.nights_to_hold` stays 1 until you approve. It assumes the two running builders (Ruth's food and water, more dead) have landed; every number must be measured again after they do. "Measured" means the lab or a read-only probe of today's game (section 5). The rest is a candidate nobody has played.

In short: a tide that grows from about 20 dead to about 75, a new voice on the radio each morning until the house is full, scrap as the one currency (it mends the wall and, at the bench, becomes shells), and a storm night to end on.

## 1. The arc

Day 1 is today's arrival (claim, fence, two survivors, a quiet night you can sleep). After that the dead come every night: night 1 is the night after the second rescue, normally the night of day 2. A day is 6 minutes of light, a night 6 (a game hour is 30 seconds): the run is about 67 minutes at normal speed (the bot's pace) and 83 at the lab's human pace, inside your 55 to 85. Points are the night's budget (walker 1, runner 2, plated brute 9); "dead" is bodies.

## 2. Economy rules

- **Scrap is the money, and the real ammo.** The workbench turns 1 scrap into 8 rounds (a person on the bench job all day, or Ruth by hand); rounds in loot (about 2 a building) only pay for the shots it took to get them. The five nights need about 190 rounds (0.8 a dead one: a shot is a third of a round, a melee kill is free). The player starts with about 46 and survivors bring 16, so 16 scrap pays for the rest.
- **Scrap in and out.** Out: the ring 117, towers and floodlights 76, reinforcing about 230, mending about 30, shells 16: about 470. In: stash, start, survivors' packs and loot give about 145. Stripping two houses a day (a house gives 11 scrap for 100 seconds of hands, a car 4 for 10; the Scavenger job does it for you) adds about 110: everything but the reinforcing. You cannot afford it all, which is the point.
- **Food and water**: 1 food and 1.5 water a day each, Ruth too (crew 3 to 6 needs 3 to 6 and 4.5 to 9). The stores at the first dusk (lab median 23 and 26) last five people about 4 days of food and 3 of water, and the barrels add 2 water a day: a player who stays home goes dry at night 4. A five-building trip brings 10 to 35 of each: from day 3 scavenging is a choice.
- **The wall comes first.** Today the bench eats scrap down to 3 and the builders do not start under 5, so the fence is never mended. New: the bench and the Build page keep 12 for the wall, builders mend anything under 70 percent each morning from the first scrap, one scrap mends 30 hp (10 today), a fallen section is rebuilt at its price (1 scrap a tile). A night's damage costs 2 to 10 scrap, not 6 to 17. The morning card lists what is worn and its cost.
- **Night size does not depend on the crew**: a fixed table (today a full house of six turns a 12-dead first night into 28). More people shoot, build, strip and eat: a bigger crew is rewarded and a small one is never punished by a smaller night.
- **One thing a morning**: a survivor on days 2 to 4 (a call each morning while a bed is free), a tip, then silence. Each opens one or two projects and brings a pack.

Lab targets (a bot that strips and mends, 64 seeds): nights 1 to 3 held 85 to 95 percent, nights 4 and 5 held 80 to 90 (the whole run won 55 to 70); an idle avatar holds nights 1 to 4 all in at most 1 run in 10; no night with a bot held share between 35 and 65 percent (a coin flip); ammo at dusk 1.2 to 3 times the night's need in 8 runs of 10; scrap at dusk 10 or more; a won run 66 to 83 real minutes.

## 4. Questions for you

1. **A survivor a day from day 2** (default yes): the only gate in the arc. A fast player no longer has all six home on day 2, as the bot does today.
2. **How hard is the finale** (default 110 points, 1.4 times night 4: the bot holds night 5 at 80 to 90 percent and wins the run 55 to 70, a person who uses the days does better). Easier 95, harder 125.
3. **If night 5 is not held** (default: the run goes on at +15 percent a night and the card says so), or defeat at once?
4. **The wall rules** (default yes): 30 hp a scrap, the bench keeps 12, a person on the bench early. Without them the lab shows the fence never mended.
5. **New things** (default none): the arc re-sequences what exists (nine projects, five survivors, stripping, the bench). Add a new thing for the last two days (a better bench or a second ring), or not?
