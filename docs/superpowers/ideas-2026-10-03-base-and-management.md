# Twenty more ideas for the base, its management and its building (2026-10-03)

> On top of the eight in the 2026-10-02 reply (the gate as the heart of the night, fuel for the base, traps, upgrades, road events, territory, a garage, a heat map). Grouped; the ones marked * are the author's picks for the next rounds.

## The yard and its buildings
1. **Wall tiers and a repair queue.** Walls wear; Builders upgrade them wood -> reinforced -> concrete; each section shows its condition and a priority.
2. **An inner line.** A second wall round the school with its own gate: a breach of the yard is a retreat, not the end. *
3. **The roof.** A school roof with a sniper's nest, solar panels and rain catchers (the first source of water and power).
4. **A real water system.** Well, barrels, a filter: water is hauled and treated, not a number; a dry spell is an event.
5. **A kitchen.** Turns raw food into meals for a morale bonus; a cook job; food spoils if it sits. *
6. **Crafting chains.** Scrap splits into metal, wood and cloth; the workbench turns them into ammo, planks, bandages and traps; trips can target a material.
7. **Storage and hauling.** Stockpile zones, a shed that protects stores from ruin and theft, haulers who move goods; caps you can see.
8. **Sanitation and sickness.** A latrine or compost; neglect starts outbreaks; a quarantine room in the infirmary; herbs in the garden. *
9. **Morale spots that are used.** The basketball court, the hopscotch, a common room with the radio, a memorial wall: people use them on breaks and it shows on their mood.

## People and routines
10. **A timetable.** Work, meals, free time and sleep for each person or each job; the night guards sleep by day; fatigue is real. *
11. **Training.** A shooting range and drills; mentors (the bonds) teach the new; apprenticeships for recruits.
12. **Roles that matter.** Engineer, medic, scout, guard with a perk each, a roster screen, one person named as leader whose decisions the crew follows.
13. **A colony journal.** "The day at a glance": who did what, what got built, who is worried; it also reads as the story of the run.

## Defence and events
14. **Zones and orders.** Mark a wall "defend first", set a rally point, put a patrol on a route by day; garrisons per section drawn in the world.
15. **Things go wrong inside.** Fire (a bucket brigade), a power cut, rain brings down a wall piece, rats in the food store, a bitten stranger at the gate: short drills that make the base feel alive. *
16. **Perimeter tech.** Alarm bells and tripwire cans, searchlights that reveal runners, a sound lure that draws the dead to the killing zone.
17. **A guard post at the gate.** A check for bites, a small quarantine cell, a toll or a trade table.

## The map and the long game
18. **Outpost kits.** A watchpost, a cache, a safehouse dropped on a place of the map; a courier route carries supplies home; a radio relay widens the board.
19. **Listening post.** Radio direction finding reveals places over days; storms and blackouts block it; mark a place as dangerous, looted or claimed, and keep a trip log.
20. **Tiers of the settlement.** Camp -> outpost -> settlement -> town, each unlocking a set of buildings and a visible look; the convoy ending (only some can leave) as the last tier. *
