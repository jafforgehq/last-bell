# Round 7: a small start, and recruits over the radio

> The user's direction (2026-10-02): the game grows from a small crew into a settlement. The crew starts at **three**; the others join over the radio; the compound's **housing** (the school's six beds plus the bunk houses of round 6) is the cap; nights follow the size of the crew. Part of `docs/superpowers/roadmap.md`. A human playtest is advised after this round.

**Goal:** a new game starts with Ruth, Dez and Marcus. At the radio hour, when someone is at the radio desk, a **call** can come: a survivor asks the way in. You tell them to come (they reach the gate at 07:00 the next morning and join the crew) or ask them to wait. The three other authored survivors (Ana, Walt, Hector) are the first callers in story order; after them callers are **generated** (name, role, look, skills, two traits, a weapon, a portrait) until the compound's beds run out. A newcomer is occasionally bitten (a few percent for the generated ones), so the gate has a decision in it. The size of the night follows the crew.

## Design

**Start mode.** `sim.crew_start` is 6 (classic) or 3 (small). In the small start `start_run` keeps Ruth, Dez and Marcus and puts the other three roster entries in the recruits' queue (their defs, in story order: Ana, Walt, Hector). `Helpers.make` and the tutorial stay classic. The new-game menu offers "Small crew (3)" (the default) and "Full crew (6)"; the command line has `--crew=3|6` (default 6 for shots and tests, so existing screenshots do not change; the menu passes the choice in the run meta as `crew`).

**Recruits** (`scripts/sim/recruits.gd`, module `sim.recruits`; data `assets/data/recruits.json`, owner ART):
- `queue`: the callers still to come: the authored defs first, then generated ones (made when needed from the module's own generator: `make_def(index) -> Dictionary`, shaped like a roster entry of `map.json`: id, name, role, age, traits (two), weapon, skills, look, portrait, `job` (where they stand when they arrive: the gate), `bitten` (a bool decided when the def is made)). At most `MAX_GENERATED` (8) generated callers in a run.
- A **call** is due at the radio hour (12:00) when the radio desk is staffed (the storyteller's `speaker()` is someone), the day is at least `FIRST_CALL_DAY` (2), the queue has someone, nobody is already coming, the last arrival or refusal was at least `gap_days()` ago (`GAP_DAYS` 3, less `radio_range()` of the buildings, at least 1; a refusal is asked again after `REFUSE_DAYS` 2), and `housing_free() >= 1` (`build.housing()` minus the people here and those coming). The module asks the storyteller to put the card `survivor_call` up (`storyteller.force`) with the caller in its context; the card's two choices are "Tell them the way in" (needs `housing`; the caller arrives at 07:00 the next day) and "Not yet".
- **Arrival:** at 07:00 `update` makes the person (`sim.add_person(def)`), at the gate (`def.job`), event `{"type": "arrived", "who": id}`, a banner ("Mia has reached the gate"), a morale lift for the crew (`ARRIVAL_MORALE` +4, +8 for a bonded person), and, if `def.bitten`, a bite (`sim.medical.add_wound(p, "bite")`) so the infection rules take over (treat with T, or send out).
- `housing_free()`, `incoming() -> Array`, `queue_size()`, `current_call() -> Dictionary` (the caller def of the card on the table), `crew_scale()`.
- **Night size follows the crew** (small start only; classic is always 1.0): `crew_scale() = clamp(0.2 + 0.8 * alive / 6, 0.5, 1.6)` where `alive` is the people not dead or turned; `waves.night_budget` multiplies by it (and `plan_for`'s cache key includes it).
- `sim.add_person(def) -> Person`: makes and registers a person (the modules that keep per-person state get `person_added(p)`: morale, traits, bonds, jobs).
- **Saves:** `recruits.save()` keeps the queue (the generated defs made so far), who is coming, the counters and the defs of the arrived recruits; `SaveGame.apply` reconciles `sim.people` with the saved people before reading them (`sim.recruits.prepare_load(data)`): the roster people the save does not list are removed, the arrived ones are made from the saved defs, in the saved order.
- **Harness:** `--crew=3` plays the small start; the policy takes the card's default (the way in).

**`recruits.json` schema (ART owns it):** `{"version": 1, "first": [...], "last": [...], "roles": [{"role", "weight", "weapon", "skills": {"Shooting": [lo, hi], ...}, "traits_bias": [ids], "line": "what the voice on the radio says", "lines_in": "what they say at the gate"}], "looks": {"skin": [...], "hair": [{"style", "colors": [...]}], "tops": [{"style", "colors"}], "outers": [...], "pants": [...], "shoes": [...], "extras": {"glasses": p, "bandolier": p}}, "portraits": ["res://assets/portraits/gen_00.png", ...], "bitten_chance": 0.08}`. The module draws every random choice from its own generator (`RandomNumberGenerator` seeded from `sim.run_seed`), so a run is repeatable.

## Acceptance

- **Rules:** a small start has three people; a call comes at noon only with someone at the radio desk and a free bed; accepting brings the caller at 07:00 next day; a refusal asks again two days later; housing stops the calls; generated callers are playable (look draws, skills, traits, weapon, portrait, a voice); a bitten arrival follows the infection rules; saves round-trip mid-run (arrived recruits included, a caller on the way included) and old saves load; nights follow the crew in the small start only; classic is unchanged (harness identical).

- **Balance (the owner checks):** the small start on Standard with the informed policy neither collapses in the first week nor is trivially won; the default policy still reaches a sensible day; classic numbers unchanged.
- **Suite green;** screenshots of the call card, an arrival at the gate, and a crew of eight in the HUD.
