# Round 4: people and the storyteller

> Follows [round 3](2026-10-01-round-3.md), the [audit](../../audit/2026-10-01-full-audit.md) (its appendix B items 5 to 11 and the spec appendix sections 4 and 5 are the sources) and the user's "yes, like that": the Town Board (the city) stays out; this round is people and a storyteller.

**Goal:** make the six survivors people and the days something other than dead time. People: two traits each, a mood that rises and falls for reasons you can read, bonds that make a loss hurt, skills that grow with use, and a voice (short lines at the right moments). Days: a radio hour at noon that brings one card a day (a boon, a threat or a choice), and a dawn assembly that tells the story of the night. It proves itself when a player can name who did what and retell a moment, and the harness shows events and mood changing nights without breaking the balance.

**Branch:** `feature/colony-sandbox`. Nothing is pushed or published. **The world art stays as it is**; the HUD and the cards use the noticeboard frames. New pictures are made with Pillow (icons, a speech-bubble frame), not new Meshy art.

Never run a dev server. Scratch files go in the session scratchpad, not in the repo.
- **Do not commit.** The owner commits. Do not push, never touch `main`.
- **Never kill processes by pattern** (`pkill`, `killall`): the user keeps the Godot editor open. **Delete only by moving to `~/.Trash`**, never `rm` (two builders slipped on this in round 3: not even for your own scratch files; use `mv` to the Trash). Do not delete anything you did not create.
- `scripts/sim/sim.gd`, `scripts/main.gd`, `scripts/ui/hud.gd` and `scripts/render/overlay_node.gd` are edited by several builders at once: **use Edit (a small string replacement), never Write over them**, re-read the lines just before you edit, keep to your own region, and do not reformat what you did not write. The new modules are already registered in `sim.gd` as neutral stubs (`traits`, `morale`, `bonds`, `skills`, `barks`, `storyteller`): you fill in your module's file; you do not re-register it.
- **Determinism:** one seeded `sim.rng`; anything that must be known before it happens comes from its own generator seeded from the run seed and the day, never from `sim.rng`. A save from this round loads back to the same game; a save from before it loads with defaults (a missing key means the default, no VERSION bump needed unless a field changes meaning).
- **Balance neutrality:** new systems start small. Constants for tuning sit in one `const` block at the top of each module. ## Design

### People (builder PEOPLE: rules)

| Piece | Rules (starting values; all tunable constants) |
|---|---|
| **Skills grow** (`skills.gd`, `Person.skills`) | Each person gets their own copy of `def.skills` (`Person.skills`, a Dictionary, saved); every use of `p.def.skills` in the rules, the view and `tools/balance.gd` reads `p.skills` instead. Credit comes through `sim.skills.credit(p, kind, amount)`: Shooting +1 per 60 kills the person makes (a kill is credited to the shooter whose shot killed), Melee +1 per 40 melee (butt of the gun) kills, Medicine +1 per 6 treatments (a cure, a stabilising or a bandage counts), Building +1 per 400 HP they mend and +1 per 60 scrap they make into ammo. At most 3 points over where they started and never past 10; Leading does not grow. `quick_learner` makes it 1.5 times faster. A gain emits `{"type": "skill_up", "who": id, "skill": String, "value": int}` and an alert. `skills.gained(p)` says how many points each skill has gained. |
| **Counters** (`Person.stats`, saved) | `kills`, `kills_night` (reset at dusk), `mended` (HP), `treated`, `crafted` (scrap), `shots`. The dawn assembly and the panel read them. |
| **Traits** (`traits.gd`, `assets/data/traits.json`) | Ten traits, each `{"id", "name", "blurb", "effects": {key: number}}`; two to a person in `map.json` (`people[].traits`). Keys the rules ask for through `traits.mult(p, key)` (product, 1.0 when none) and `traits.add(p, key)` (sum): `aim`, `reload` (time), `repair`, `craft`, `bandage` (speed), `wound` (chance a claw or bite wounds them), `dead_morale` (how much the dead at the wall shake them), `morale_recover`, `grief`, `xp` (multipliers); `cure`, `bond` (additive). The ten: **Steady** dead_morale 0.5; **Nervous** dead_morale 1.5 and aim 0.9 while 6 or more of the dead are within 10 tiles; **Tinkerer** repair 1.3, craft 1.2; **Triage** bandage 1.3, cure +0.1; **Sharp-eyed** aim 1.08; **Steady hands** reload 0.75; **Tough** wound 0.75; **Optimist** morale_recover 1.5; **Loyal** grief 1.5, bond +0.04; **Quick learner** xp 1.5. Suggested: Ruth Steady and Loyal, Dez Tinkerer and Tough, Ana Triage and Optimist, Marcus Nervous and Quick learner, Walt Steady hands and Sharp-eyed, Hector Tough and Steady. Wire each key into the one place the rules use it (aim and reload in `sim._fire/_combat`, repair in `sim._update_person`, craft in `workshop.gd`, bandage and cure in `medical.gd`, wound in the claw and bite rolls). |
| **Bonds** (`bonds.gd`, `map.json` `bonds`) | A list `{"a": id, "b": id, "kind": "friend" or "family" or "mentor"}`: Ruth and Marcus (family: she is his principal), Walt and Marcus (mentor), Dez and Hector (friend), Ana and Ruth (friend). Within 6 tiles of a bonded person someone aims 8% better (`bond` adds to it); a bonded person who is downed, dead, turned or exiled is a shock (see morale). `bonds.of(p)`, `bonds.near(p, tiles)`. |
| **Morale** (`morale.gd`) | Each person has a value 0 to 100, starting at 70, that drifts 4 points an hour toward a target, `60` plus the continuous modifiers: well fed and watered +5; hungry -8, starving -20, thirsty -8, parched -20; bleeding -6; infected -8; downed -15; near a bonded person +5; the leader (Ruth, Leading 9) within 10 tiles + (Leading x 0.6, at most 6, only if she is up); the dead clawing at a wall within 10 tiles -0.4 each (at most -12, scaled by `dead_morale`); a quiet night (no dead within 15 tiles after the bell) +2. Shocks (instant, then they drift away): a kill +0.2 (at most +6 a night), a wall breach -5 for all, a repair finished +2 for the repairer, a friend downed -10, a friend dead or turned -25 (times `grief`), anyone else dead -8 for all, an exile -6 for all and -15 for friends; the storyteller adds its own. `mood(p)`: 80 and over Inspired (everything x1.05), 40 to 79 Steady, 20 to 39 Shaken (work x0.9, aim x0.9), under 20 Breaking (work x0.75). Under 10 a person has a **breakdown** for one game hour (they stop shooting and cannot be ordered, an alert says so) and afterwards sit at 35. Breakdowns are off on Story difficulty and before day 4. `morale.perf(p)` goes into `sim.perf`; `morale.explain(p)` lists every modifier with its value for the panel; `status_of(p)` gives a mark while Shaken, Breaking or in a breakdown. `shock(null, delta, label)` is for everyone. Saved. |
| **Barks** (`barks.gd`, `assets/data/barks.json`) | `{person id: {context: [lines]}}`. Contexts: `bell` (dusk), `kill_streak` (five kills in a minute), `wounded`, `low_ammo`, `friend_down`, `breach`, `dawn` (survived the night), `skill_up`, `breakdown`, `epitaph` (said about them after they die: one line, for the dawn assembly). At least four lines for each of the first eight contexts and two epitaphs for each of the six; a voice for each (Ruth firm and dry, Dez practical and wry, Ana gentle and tired, Marcus young and quick, Walt dry and old-school, Hector quiet and kind). Rate: at most one bark a person every 25 s and one anywhere every 6 s, never during a modal or the tutorial; `say(p, context)` picks a line they did not use last time. It adds `{"type": "bark", "who": id, "text", "context"}` events. |

### People (builder PEOPLE-UI: view)

The person panel gets a **Traits** row (chips with tooltips from `traits.of(p)`), a **Mood** row (the word, a small bar and a tooltip listing `morale.explain(p)` with signs), a **Bonds** line (names and kinds, tooltip) and the skills show gains ("Shooting 8 +1"); stats (kills, mended HP, treated) go in a tooltip. The layout does not grow past what fits: the dock and its nine order buttons must still fit at 700, 980 and 1280 wide and in short windows (the HUD tests sweep them). The crew cards show a small mood mark when someone is Shaken or Breaking (through the status marks); a dead person's card reads "In memory" and its tooltip gives their epitaph. **Speech bubbles** in `overlay_node.gd`: a bark shows as a small bubble above the person for about 3 s (a `bubble` frame added to `tools/make_ui_theme.py` and `assets/ui/theme/frames.json`, flat fallback without it), stays inside the view, fades (no fade under `Prefs.on("ui","reduce_motion")`), at most two at once. Alert icons for the new alert ids. Tests against the stub modules and, once they land, the real ones.

### The storyteller (builder STORY: rules and cards)

| Piece | Rules |
|---|---|
| **The radio hour** (`storyteller.gd`) | At 12:00 each day (Ruth's job at the radio made real: if Ruth is gone or down, whoever has the best Leading speaks) the storyteller may draw a card: about two days in three, never on day 1 (a quiet start) or on day 30 (the Big Night); at most two threat cards in a row; the same card not again within 6 days. A card is `{"id", "kind": "boon" or "threat" or "choice", "weight", "min_day", "max_day", "title", "text", "choices": [{"label", "hint", "effects", "result"}], "bot": index}` in `assets/data/events.json`. The draw leans on a hidden **tension** (0 to 100): it rises with trouble (a breach, a death, a threat card, a bad night) and falls 8 a quiet day; under 20 threats are twice as likely, over 70 boons are. The draw comes from its own generator seeded from the run seed and the day. The style follows the difficulty: Story is Calm (threat weights x0.6), Standard Steady, Brutal x1.5 threats and x0.7 boons. A card with a choice waits for the player (the rules pause while the radio card is open); with no choice it resolves at once and the card shows what happened. `pending()`, `choose(i)` (for the harness and the view), and if a card is still pending 2 game hours later the `bot` choice is taken. |
| **The deck** | About 14 cards at launch, each with a real consequence the player can see: **Supply drop** (a crate in the yard: fetch it with two people, who are away until 16:00 and may bring walkers back, or leave it); **Someone at the gate** (a stranger: let them in, trade across the gate or send them away: they may bring 4 meds, steal 10 food and water, or be bitten and turn in the night inside the yard); **A cry at the gate** (at 18:00, someone runs for the gate with two of the dead behind: open it, cover them from the wall, or leave them; each choice costs something and morale moves); **Trade on the radio** (5 meds for 60 ammo, yes or no); **Heavy rain** (barrels fill, +20 water); **Fog** (tonight the guns reach 30% less far); **Blackout** (the generator fails at 21:00 and the lamps stay off until Dez has fixed it: send him now or sit in the dark; in the dark aim is -25% beyond 6 tiles); **Stragglers** (at 14:00 6 + day/3 of the dead arrive at one wall by day: a daytime alarm); **Fever** (one person is sick for a day: work x0.75, Ana can treat it for one medicine); **Mould** (-15% of the food); **Wall rot** (a wall section loses 30% of its health); **Horde warning** (tonight's budget x1.3: the 17:00 forecast says so); **A quiet day** (+8 morale for all); **Old friends on the radio** (a morale lift for one person, chosen by their role); **An argument** (Dez and Hector fall out: -5 morale for both unless Ruth steps in, a choice). Choices carry risk the card shows plainly ("a 1 in 4 chance of a bite"). The builder may swap, add or drop cards to keep about fourteen good ones; every one is data plus effects from the vocabulary below, and has a test. |
| **Effect vocabulary** | `stock` {food, water, scrap, ammo, meds: delta}; `morale` {who: "all" or an id or a role, delta, label}; `wound` {who, kind}; `sick` {who: "random" or an id, hours}; `weather` ("fog", "rain", "clear" for the coming night); `blackout` {from_hour, until: "fixed"}; `night_budget` {multiplier} (the plan and the forecast include it: `waves.plan_for` is recomputed when it changes); `spawn_day` {count, at: "north", "gate" or a section}; `wall_damage` {section: "random", fraction}; `party` (send people out like a scavenging trip, through the scavenge module); `chance` {p, then: [...], else: [...]} drawing from `sim.rng`; `tension` {delta}. |
| **The dawn assembly** (`report()` and a card) | Built at each dawn from the counters: the night's number, how many were put down, the MVP (most kills: `Person.stats.kills_night`), the wall health lost and mended, who was lost (with their epitaph bark), skill gains, the events of the day, and the mood of the crew in a few words. It is shown as a paper card on the HUD that does **not** pause the game (it stays 15 s or until clicked or any key, and is reachable again from the alert feed); it blocks nobody. |
| **Cards in the view** | `scripts/ui/radio_card.gd` (a paper modal like `orientation_card.gd`: a small radio icon made with Pillow, the speaker, the text, the choices as buttons with their hint and risk, keyboard 1 to 3) and `scripts/ui/dawn_card.gd`, opened from `main.gd` on the `story_card` and `dawn_report` events (narrow edits in `_handle_events`). Weather and lighting: `fog` and the blackout in `lighting.gd` (lamps off, a cooler tint, a fog veil), without touching the world art. |
| **Harness** | `tools/balance.gd` Policy: when a card is pending it takes `choices[bot]`; a test. |

## Interfaces (the stubs in `scripts/sim/` carry them)

- `sim.traits`: `of(p) -> Array` of `{"id", "name", "blurb"}`; `mult(p, key) -> float`; `add(p, key) -> float`.
- `sim.morale`: `enabled`; `value(p) -> float`; `mood(p) -> String` ("Inspired", "Steady", "Shaken", "Breaking"); `explain(p) -> Array` of `{"label", "value"}`; `breakdown(p) -> bool`; `perf(p) -> float`; `shock(p_or_null, delta, label)`; `status_of(p) -> Array`.
- `sim.bonds`: `of(p) -> Array` of `{"who": Person, "kind"}`; `near(p, tiles) -> bool`.
- `sim.skills`: `gained(p) -> Dictionary`; `credit(p, kind, amount)`. `Person.skills: Dictionary`, `Person.stats: Dictionary`.
- `sim.barks`: `say(p, context)`.
- `sim.storyteller`: `enabled`; `tension`; `pending() -> Dictionary`; `choose(i) -> bool`; `report() -> Dictionary`.
- Events: `{"type": "bark", "who", "text", "context"}`, `{"type": "skill_up", "who", "skill", "value"}`, `{"type": "story_card"}`, `{"type": "story_resolved", "id", "text"}`, `{"type": "dawn_report"}`.
- Alert ids: `skill-<id>`, `breakdown-<id>`, `story`, `story-<id>`, `dawn`.

## Acceptance

- **People:** a person who shoots 60 times has Shooting +1; traits change the numbers they claim; a friend's death is a shock to those who knew them; low morale costs aim and work and breakdowns happen rarely (not at all on Story or before day 4); barks show at the right moments and never in a modal; every modifier is visible in a tooltip.
- **Storyteller:** a card most days from day 2, never two threats more than twice running, never the same card within 6 days; every card resolves and changes the state it says it changes; a card left unanswered resolves by its `bot` choice after 2 game hours; tomorrow is not knowable (the draw is seeded from the run seed and the day) but a game plays the same way from the same seed and orders; the dawn assembly's numbers match the counters; fog and blackout change the night and the look.
- **Tutorial, saves, determinism:** Orientation Day is unaffected (the whole tutorial suite stays green); a save round-trips with the new state; a pre-round-4 save loads with defaults.
- **Balance (the owner checks with the harness, Standard, 30 days, 16 seeds):** the default policy's median stays between 16 and 22, the informed policy wins between 20% and 45% of games, hands-off 10 to 14, Story informed at least 70% wins; if any of that moves, the owner retunes the constants.
- **The suite is green;** screenshots of the person panel with traits and mood, a bubble, a radio card, the dawn card, a blackout night and a foggy one.

## Result (2026-10-01)

All four tasks are done and committed on `feature/colony-sandbox` (the plan and stubs `f53c368`, the built work `8d9e7b5`, then the tuning, the three small view fixes and the docs). Section 7f of the spec records the numbers.

Tuned with the harness (Standard, 30 days): default median day 17.5 over 16 seeds, the informed policy wins 6 of 16 (it won 8 of 16 as first built, before the traits were cut to about half), hands-off 11, Story informed 7 of 8, Brutal 0 of 8. Added by the builders beyond the plan: a person in a breakdown shows "Having a breakdown" and every order but Follow says why it cannot be given; `--card=`, `--weather=`, `--blackout` and `--dawn` screenshot flags; `--nostory` for the harness; the HUD knows `hud.modal_open()`.

Not done, on purpose: the study hall (a teacher trains a student), the Last-Bell Knock as a real runner at the gate (the "cry at the gate" card stands in for it), scavenging in beats and scouting, wall sockets, and the crew draft: all later. The dawn card in a very short 700-wide window can still touch the dock's top row.
