# Round 13: vehicles and fuel

> The user's idea (2026-10-02): "add vehicles when visiting another city and add fuel so we can fill up fuel in a car and send folks in another city." Part of docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-02-rounds-11-14.md.

**Goal:** the Town Board gets a way to go far and fast: **vehicles** found in the town (a sedan, a pickup, a van, a bus), **fuel** found at the gas stations and the garage, and a **second place to go, across the river** (a city of about ten places, reachable only by vehicle). A party that takes a vehicle makes the road in a third of the hours, carries more (up to its seats) and more loot (the vehicle's cargo), and costs fuel; a vehicle can be damaged or lost on a bad trip. Settlement game only (`sim.town.on`); the classic start is untouched.

## Design

**Fuel** is a store of its own (`sim.vehicles.fuel`, capped), not one of the five stocks (so saves and the classic stores do not change): found as loot (`fuel` in a place's `loot`, paid in by `scavenge`), spent by trips. The HUD's stores panel shows a Fuel line in the settlement game once there is a vehicle or any fuel.

**Vehicles** (`assets/data/vehicles.json`, module `scripts/sim/vehicles.gd`, `sim.vehicles`): kinds with `name`, `seats`, `speed` (the road's hours are divided by it), `burn` (fuel for each hour driven), `cargo` (the loot's multiplier), `hp`, and the picture to draw. A place of the town may hold one (`vehicle: "pickup"` in town.json); looting or clearing it, the party that survives has a chance (0.5 plus 0.05 for each point of Building in the party, at most 0.95) to get it running and drive it home: it joins the fleet, parked at the gate. A vehicle is **parked**, **out** with a party or **lost**.

**Trips by vehicle:** `scavenge.send(people, place, action, vehicle_id)`: the party must fit the seats (more than the three of a party on foot, up to six), the fuel must cover `burn x hours driven` (twice the one-way hours at the vehicle's speed), and the vehicle must be parked. The trip's hours are `2 x one way / speed + the search`; the loot is multiplied by the cargo; the vehicle returns with the party (parked again), with `hp` less for the place's danger and the wounded, and is lost when the party is lost or its `hp` runs out. **A place that `needs_vehicle` cannot be visited on foot** ("Too far to go on foot: take a vehicle").

**The city across the river:** the board grows from 100 to 160 tiles wide; about ten places on its east side (a hospital, a mall, a university, the police headquarters, a rail yard, a power station, an airfield's fuel depot, a harbour...) with `needs_vehicle`, reached over a long road across a bridge (a place on the board, `kind: bridge`, known after scouting the east). They are richer and more dangerous than the town's, and shown on the same map (the Town Board scales the board to fit).

## Acceptance

- Rules: a party by car takes the hours and fuel the data says, fits its seats, carries the cargo's loot, the vehicle comes home (or is lost) with it; a place that needs a vehicle is refused on foot; fuel is found as loot and capped; a vehicle is found by looting its place; saves round-trip mid-trip with a vehicle and with the fleet; the classic start is unchanged.
- Screens: the party picker offers the vehicles and shows the hours, the fuel and the seats; the city is on the map; the stores show fuel; the gate shows the parked vehicles; at 700 to 1920 wide with nothing overlapping or under 12 px.
- Balance: the settlement game stays winnable; the bot that uses vehicles brings more home than the one that walks.
