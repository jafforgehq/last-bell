# Round 2: the night as a puzzle

> Follows [round 0](2026-10-01-round-0.md) and the [audit](../../audit/2026-10-01-full-audit.md). The user said "do that" to: runners and brutes, lures, a fields-of-fire view, standing orders and the Big Night.

**Goal:** make a night something the player can read and solve: information (a forecast, a coverage view), positioning (where the shooters stand), a way to steer the dead (the lure), threats that ask for different answers (runners, brutes), less chores (standing orders, repair speed from skill) and a climax (the Big Night). It proves itself when an informed crew beats a naive one by days.

**Branch:** `feature/colony-sandbox`. Nothing is pushed or published. The world art stays as it is; new drawings are the same figures scaled and tinted, no new art style.

## Global constraints

- Godot 4.7.2, GDScript. The suite stays green; new behaviour gets a test that failed first.
- Deterministic: one seeded `sim.rng`; anything that must be known earlier than it happens (tonight's plan, for the forecast) comes from its own generator seeded by `hash([run_seed, day])`, never from `sim.rng`.
- A save from before this round may be refused (VERSION bump); a save from this round loads back to the same game.
- World art untouched; HUD and menus keep the dark style (the noticeboard re-skin is round 3).

## Design

| Piece | Rules |
|---|---|
| Kinds of the dead (`scripts/sim/kinds.gd`) | **Walker**: hp 4-7, speed 0.5-0.85, cost 1. **Runner** (from night 4): hp 2-4, speed 1.5-2.1, claws walls at 0.8, cost 2, never limps. **Brute** (from night 8): hp 22-30, speed 0.42-0.6, claws walls at 3.5x, cost 9. Shares of a night's budget: runners 6% at night 4 rising 3% a night to 30%; brutes 8% at night 8 rising 2% a night to 20%. A brute is shot first, a runner second. |
| Tonight's plan (`waves.gd`) | At dusk (and for the forecast at 17:00) the night is planned from `hash([run_seed, day])`: the queue of kinds, and one or two **main spawn points** that bring 65% of the dead. The plan is the same whenever it is computed. |
| The forecast (`tactics.gd`) | At 17:00, if the lookout (Marcus) is alive, here and not down: a banner and an alert "about N tonight (+-15%), mostly from the north east, a brute is coming". If he is not, nobody tells the crew. |
| The lure (`tactics.gd`) | A wall section can be set as the **alarm** (key L, 3 scrap, 3 game hours, one at a time): slots on it score 40 tiles better; the dead already on their way and not yet clawing switch to it with 70% chance when a place is free. |
| Standing orders (`tactics.gd`) | **Auto-repair** (key B, a toggle on a person): by day, while scrap is 5 or more and nobody else is on it, they repair the most worn section under 70%. On for Dez and Hector at the start. |
| Building speed | A repair mends 5 + Building points a second (Dez 14, Marcus 6), was a flat 9. |
| Fields of fire (view only) | Key V shows, over every wall section, how many shooters at their night posts can reach it: none red, one amber, two or more green; plus the lure and the main spawn points on the forecast night. `tactics.coverage_of(seg)`. |
| The Big Night | Night 30: budget x2.4, runners 30% and brutes 25% of it, the cap on the dead alive 90; a banner and an alert at dusk; dawn is the victory. |
| Informed policy (harness) | `--policy=informed`: the default policy plus: stand the shooters where they cover the thinnest section and away from the wall (3+ tiles), set the lure on the best-covered section when a night starts. |

## Interfaces (so builders and the owner can work apart)

Rules side (owner), already in place before the builders start:

- `Zombie.kind: String` ("walker", "runner", "brute"), `Zombie.size: float`, `Zombie.claw: float`; `Kinds.KINDS`.
- `sim.tactics` (module): `lure_seg() -> Segment or null`, `lure_left_min() -> float`, `order_lure(seg) -> bool`, `auto_repair(p) -> bool`, `toggle_auto(p)`, `coverage_of(seg) -> int`, `coverage_map() -> Dictionary` (segment id to count), `why_not_lure(seg) -> String`, `forecast() -> Dictionary` (see `tactics.gd`; its `spawns` are the main spawn points as Vector2).
- Gizmos through `sim.handle_gizmo(id, sel)`: `lure` (a wall section selected), `auto` (a person selected). `hud.gd` builds their buttons.
- Events: `{"type": "groan", "x", "y", "kind"}` (kind for the pitch), `{"type": "lure", "seg": id}`.

## Acceptance

- Kinds: a runner crosses the map in under half the walker's time; a brute does about three and a half times the wall damage per claw and takes many shots (hp at least three times a walker's best); day 1 has only walkers; night 30 has the multiplier.
- The plan and the forecast agree (the forecast number is within 15% of what the night then brings, with Marcus alive); the forecast is silent without him.
- The lure moves the dead: with it set, most of those on their way end at the lured section; it costs 3 scrap and ends after 3 game hours.
- Auto-repair mends worn sections by day without orders and stops when scrap is under 5.
- `--policy=informed` beats the default policy by at least 3 days at the median over 16 seeds (if not, say so with the data).
- The full suite is green; screenshots of the coverage view, a brute, a runner and the lure marker look right.

## Result (2026-10-01)

All six tasks are done and committed on `feature/colony-sandbox` (rules `9e21699`, harness `34bfcc3`, drawing `535a273`, HUD `0823443`, then the docs). The suite is 507 tests, 0 failures.

Harness, Standard, 30 days, medians of the day reached: no orders 11 (seeds 1-8), the default policy 17 (16 seeds), the informed policy 24 (16 seeds), so the informed bot beats the naive one by seven days (the acceptance was three). On Story the informed policy wins 5 of 8 games and the default policy 2 of 8; nobody reaches day 30 on Standard (the informed crew runs out of ammo around day 22 to 26).

What changed against the plan: brute hit points 22 to 30 and claw 3.5 times (not 26 to 36 and 4), starting scrap 40 and the hardware store 18 to 34 scrap (the stock was gone by day 5 with brutes tearing walls down), the alarm's pull is 40 tiles (26 drew too few of the dead), and the forecast is read from `forecast().spawns` (there is no separate `main_spawns()`). Added by the builders: the selected-walker panel names the kind, the alarm rings a higher bell, brutes and runners groan at a lower and higher pitch, and the screenshot flags `--fields`, `--lure=N` and `--wall=N`.

Not done, on purpose: the groan pitches have not been heard by a person, and real mouse play in a live window is untested (the HUD tests click synthetically). Round 3 owns the workshop (ammo from scrap), the title screen and menus, the noticeboard re-skin and the tutorial.
