## Shared conventions (all builders)

- **Times are game hours** unless a name says seconds. One game hour is 20 real seconds at normal speed. Write thresholds in game hours and convert with `sim.game_hours(dt)`.
- **Person state** (fields on `Person`, all added already): `dead` (out of the game for good: bled out, starved, exiled or turned), `turned` (became a zombie), `away` (a scavenging party or exile record: the person is off the map and is not drawn), `downed` (at 0 health, or resting in bed after being stabilised), `hunger_h` and `thirst_h` (game hours since a proper meal or drink), `bleeding` (health lost per game hour) and `wounds` (open wounds), `bleed_out` (game hours left while downed and not stabilised, else -1), `stabilised`, `infected_at` and `turn_at` (`sim.clock_min` values, else -1), `resting_bed` (a `Vector2` while healing in bed, else null). Code that loops over `sim.people` for actions must skip `dead` and `away` ones; the zombies already do.
- **What people are doing:** set `p.activity` to a short phrase ("Bandaging Marcus", "Resting in the infirmary", "Heading for the gate"); the HUD shows it.
- **Cross-module calls:** `sim.medical.add_wound(p, "scratch" | "bite")` and `sim.medical.kill(p, cause)` (scavenging uses them for injuries and losses), `sim.medical.on_downed(p, cause)` (supplies uses it if starvation brings someone to 0), `sim.stock[...]` for loot and costs (never below 0), `sim.alert(...)`.
- **Difficulty** is `sim.difficulty` (0 story, 1 standard, 2 brutal). Multipliers by difficulty: infection chance `[0.3, 0.6, 1.0]`, scavenging danger `[0.5, 1.0, 1.6]`.
- **Saves:** each module's `save()` returns plain data (numbers, strings, arrays, dictionaries; no objects) and `load_state(d)` restores it on a freshly built sim. Refer to people by `id` and to wall sections by their `id`.
- **New event types** you add go in the events list with a short doc comment at the top of your module; main.gd ignores unknown events.

## Review focus (the lead checks these at the end)

1. No stock ever goes negative; nothing is eaten, spent or looted twice; a person who is `away`, `dead` or `turned` is never given orders, eaten for, treated or hunted.
2. The clock math is right across midnight and across step sizes (meal times fire once; timers use `clock_min`).
3. Every state machine ends: a patient always gets treated, dies or recovers; a party always comes back or is settled; no one is stuck lying down or away forever.
4. Saves: a save at any moment reloads to a state that then plays out identically.
5. The HUD never shows stale or wrong numbers, never overlaps at 1280, 980 and 700 px wide, and every new button works.

## Task S: Supplies (supplies builder)

Fill in `scripts/sim/supplies.gd`. Spec section 4.1. Starting numbers are starting values; put them in named constants at the top of the file.

- **Meals.** At 07:00, 13:00 and 19:00 (once each per day, however the steps fall; use a per-day slot counter, not an equality test on the time), each living person who is not `away` takes 1/3 food and 1/2 water from `sim.stock`. A person who gets at least a quarter of the food share resets `hunger_h` to 0; one who gets at least a quarter of the water share resets `thirst_h`. If the stock runs out mid-meal, people are served in crew order and the rest go without. Raise a short banner at meal times only if stock is low (no spam).
- **Hunger and thirst** accrue with game time for every living person not `away` (`hunger_h += sim.game_hours(dt)`; away people are on trip rations: their counters hold still). `perf(p)` returns 0.8 once `hunger_h >= 18` or `thirst_h >= 14`, else 1.0. At `hunger_h >= 36` or `thirst_h >= 24` the person loses 1 health per game hour; at 0 health call `sim.medical.on_downed(p, "hunger")` or `"thirst"`.
- **Sources.** At 06:30 each day (when the day begins): roll the day's `sim.weather` ("rain" with probability 1/3 from `sim.rng`, else "clear"); the vegetable beds give +3 food (once per day); the two rain barrels give +2 water, or +10 on a rainy day. Each needs at least one living person on the map. (Supply drops and scavenging are other modules' sources.)
- **Repairs cost scrap.** `repair_budget(amount)` returns how much of the wanted health points of repair may be done now: each 10 points cost 1 scrap; it deducts the scrap it uses and returns 0 when the stock is empty. When scrap hits 0 while someone is trying to repair, raise the alert `no-scrap` ("No scrap: repairs stopped", level "warn", ttl 8).
- **Low stock alerts:** `days_left("food")` or `("water")` under 2 raises an alert (`low-food`, `low-water`; "Food is running out", "about N days left"); do not repeat more often than every game hour.
- **`days_left(kind)`:** stock divided by the living crew's daily use (1.0 food, 1.5 water a person a day); counts people not `dead`; returns 99.0 if nobody is alive.
- **`status_of(p)`:** `[{"icon": "hungry", "color": Color, "tip": "Hungry"}]`, `"thirsty"` and (severe) `"starving"`; empty when fine.
- **`save()` / `load_state()`:** the meal slot counter, the day of the last source roll, flags for alerts.
- **`START_STOCK`** in `sim.gd`: you may tune the water/food starting numbers after checking that an unmanaged crew reaches about day 6 before anyone goes hungry with no income, and that income alone does not make the game easy; say what you chose.

**Tests** (`tests/test_supplies.gd`): meals take exactly 1/3 food and 1/2 water a person at the three times and only once per day, at any step size; hunger and thirst accrue and the 0.8 penalty and health loss start at the right hours; a short stock serves the first people and leaves the rest hungry; away people neither eat nor starve; the vegetable beds and barrels give the right amounts once a day and rain is about one day in three over many seeds; repairs cost 1 scrap per 10 points and stop at 0 with the alert; `days_left`; the statuses; starvation reaches `on_downed`; stocks never go negative in a fuzz loop of 20 random seeds over 5 days; `save`/`load_state` round trip.

**Commit:** "Supplies: meals, thirst, sources and paid repairs".

## Task M: Medical (medical builder)

Fill in `scripts/sim/medical.gd`. Spec section 4.2. Keep the defaults' old behaviour working until each part lands.

- **Wounds.** `add_wound(p, kind)`: a `"bite"` adds a wound that bleeds 4 health per game hour, a `"scratch"` one that bleeds 2; `p.wounds` counts them and `p.bleeding` is the sum. Each step a bleeding person loses `bleeding × sim.game_hours(dt)` health. `after_bite(z, p)` calls `add_wound(p, "bite")`. `on_wall_claw(z, seg)`: each claw has a 4% chance to scratch one person standing within 1.5 tiles of the clawing zombie on the inside of the wall (a wound of kind "scratch", never infection; blood fx; alert `scratch-<id>` "X was scratched").
- **Infection.** A bite infects with the difficulty's chance (`[0.3, 0.6, 1.0]`) if the person is not already infected: `infected_at = sim.clock_min`, `turn_at = infected_at + (12 + 12 × rng.randf()) × 60` game minutes. `infection_stage(p)`: `""` until 6 game hours after infection, `"feverish"` until 10, then `"delirious"`. When `clock_min >= turn_at` the person **turns**: `p.dead = true; p.turned = true`; call `sim.spawn(p.x, p.y, "breach")` and set the zombie's `turned_name`; a danger alert and a banner ("Walt has turned"); a person resting in bed turns in bed.
- **Downed.** `on_downed(p, cause)`: `downed = true`, `moving = false`, `bleed_out = 3.0` game hours, the `down-<id>` alert ("X is down", "Get them to Ana" while Ana is able, else "Stabilise them"). While downed and not stabilised `bleed_out` counts down. At 0: with `sim.permadeath` the person dies (`kill(p, "bled out")`); without it they are stabilised automatically and taken to bed.
- **Treating (automatic for bleeding and the downed).** Every step, find patients: downed and not stabilised first, then bleeding people (`bleeding > 0`). Assign the nearest free healer with `Medicine >= 1`, preferring higher Medicine (Ana first); a healer is free if not dead, downed, away, drafted with an order, or already treating. `goal_for(p)` returns `{"x", "y", "face": "", "what": "Bandaging Marcus"}` for the patient's position for an assigned healer (the rules walk them there and call `goal_for` again each step). When the healer is within 0.9 tiles: a bandage takes 10 game minutes and 1 meds (no meds: an alert `no-meds` "No medicine" and they wait; do not spam); it sets `bleeding` and `wounds` to 0. A downed patient is **stabilised** the same way (`stabilised = true`, `bleed_out = -1`), then carried to a bed: take the nearest free bed (`cot`s first, then the bunk `mattress`es, read from `sim.d.objects`; if none, stay put), set `x`, `y` to it, `resting_bed` and `activity = "Resting in the infirmary"`. Resting heals 10 health per game hour; at 40 health they get up (`downed = false`, `resting_bed = null`) and return to duty.
- **Treating infection (by the player's order).** `gizmo("treat", sel)` on an infected person: a healer is assigned; treatment takes 15 game minutes and 2 meds; success chance is `min(0.9, 0.3 + 0.1 × Medicine)` of the healer; on success `infected_at = turn_at = -1`; on failure the meds are spent and it can be ordered again while time remains. `can_treat(p)` is true for someone bleeding, downed and unstabilised, or infected.
- **Sending out.** `gizmo("sendout", sel)` on an infected person: they walk to the gate (segment 2 centre), then leave the map for good (`away = {"kind": "exile"}`, `dead = true`), with an alert "X left through the gate". `can_send_out(p)` is true for someone infected.
- **Permadeath off** (`sim.permadeath = false`): nobody dies of bleeding; see Downed.
- **`kill(p, cause)`:** `dead = true`, `downed = true`, an alert `dead-<id>` ("X died", the cause), a banner.
- **`perf(p)`:** 0.85 when health is under 40, else 1.0.
- **`status_of(p)`:** marks for `"wound"` (bleeding), `"fever"` (infected, feverish or delirious), `"down"` (downed), `"resting"`, each with a colour and tip.
- **`save()` / `load_state()`:** assigned healers, patients' timers, anything not on the people.
- The drawing already shows `downed` as lying; a person resting in bed or dead should look right (lying, at the bed); fix `pawn_node.gd` if not.

**Tests** (`tests/test_medical.gd`): a bite starts a wound and bleeding, bleeds health at the right rate, and infects at about the difficulty's chance over many seeds; a scratch never infects; a healer with Medicine is assigned, walks over and bandages (costs 1 meds; bleeding stops); no meds means no bandage and one alert; a downed person is stabilised in time and ends up resting in bed and getting up at 40 health; left alone they die after 3 game hours (permadeath) or are saved (permadeath off); infection stages and the turn at 12 to 24 hours create a named zombie in "breach" state and mark the person `turned`; treating an infected person costs 2 meds and succeeds at about the stated rate; sending out walks to the gate and removes them; `perf`; nobody `away` or `dead` is treated; save round trip; a seeded 10-day run with bites injected never leaves anyone stuck.

**Commit:** "Medical: wounds, healing, the downed and infection".
