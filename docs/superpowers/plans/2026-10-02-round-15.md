## The picture of a car trip (the contract between tasks)

**Geometry** is data: `assets/data/motor_pool.json` (the owner wrote a first version; task GATE owns its numbers, everybody else only reads it through `scripts/sim/convoy.gd`).
- The **motor pool** is an apron just inside the main gate (x 35.2 to 42.8, y 19.8 to 32): up to 4 parking spots and an aisle to the gate. Vehicles no longer park in the street.
- The **gate** is the 6-tile opening in the north wall at x 36 to 42 (wall segment 2). The street outside is rows 6 to 12 (asphalt); the **eastbound lane** (south side) has its centre at y 11.25. A trip leaves through the gate, turns right (east) into that lane and drives off the map's east edge (x 90); it returns from the west edge (x -8) along the same lane, turns right (south) into the gate and parks. So cars never cross each other.
- Every spot has `out_route` and `in_route`: polylines of points `{x, y, r, rev, h}`: `r` is the corner radius in tiles; `rev: true` marks a stretch driven in reverse (the nose keeps its heading while the car moves backwards); `h` (optional, degrees, 0 = east, 90 = south, 270 = north, the nose's heading at that point, interpolated between points with the angles unwrapped) is for a hand-tuned swing such as a U-turn. **The cars park nose to the north and leave forward. They arrive forward through the gate, make a U-turn in the top of the apron and back into their spot** (the way a motor pool parks), so the arrival route is: lane, gate, forward into the apron, a swing round to face north, a reversing stretch down the aisle and into the bay. `scripts/sim/convoy.gd` (task TRIPS) turns a route into a smooth path: `Convoy.route(spot, "out"|"in")`, `Convoy.length(route)`, `Convoy.pose(route, progress)` returning `{pos: Vector2 (the car's centre, tiles), heading: float (radians, 0 = pointing east, the nose), reverse: bool}` for progress 0 to 1, and `Convoy.door_spot(spot, vehicle_kind, door_id)` returning the tile where a person stands to use that door (outside the car, beside the door).

**Vehicle pictures and doors** are data in `assets/data/vehicles.json`: per kind `nose` (which way the picture's nose points when drawn upright: "N", "S" or "E") and `doors`: a list of `{id, side ("L" or "R" looking along the nose), seats: [seat indexes that use it], panel: [x, y, w, h] in the picture's pixels, hinge: [x, y] in the picture's pixels, open_deg: how far it swings (positive = clockwise as drawn)}`. Task DOORS makes the pictures: `assets/objects/<art>_open.png` (the body with every door's panel replaced by the dark cabin) and `assets/objects/<art>_door_<id>.png` (each door panel, cut from the picture, transparent elsewhere, same canvas size as the body picture so it can be drawn at the same position and rotated about `hinge`). Drawn at angle 0 on the `_open` body they must reproduce the original picture (test: within a small tolerance). A closed, still car is drawn with the original picture; while any door moves it is drawn as `_open` plus the panels.

**The sim's states** (task FLEET owns them; the view only reads them). A party sent by vehicle has `vehicle` (an id) and `state`:
`"out"` (walking to the doors: each member's goal is `Convoy.door_spot(...)`) -> `"driving_out"` (everybody is aboard; `drive_t` of `drive_len` game minutes along `out_route`) -> `"away"` (as today, the trip's hours) -> `"driving_in"` (the car arrives along `in_route`; same two fields) -> `"unloading"` (members get out one by one) -> done (`_come_back` as today). While `"out"` and `"unloading"`, `party.doors` is a list of `{pid, door, t, dur, dir}` for each person at a door (`dir`: "in" or "out"; `t` and `dur` in game minutes): the door is open from the start of the entry to its end, and the person disappears into (or appears from) the car at the middle. Persons who are in a car are `p.away = party` as today (hidden). A vehicle record has `spot` (an index into the motor pool, kept when it is added and when it comes back). The sim never moves a car itself: the view computes the pose from `drive_t / drive_len`, so the animation is a pure function of the saved state (a save and a load in the middle of a trip look right). A trip on foot is unchanged (people walk to the gate point and vanish; the gate opens for them).
The game's own speed scales everything (it is game minutes), so the animation is quick at speed 3 and slow at 1.

**The gate**: `scripts/render/gate_node.gd` (task GATE) is the two leaves of the gate (`assets/objects/165_gate_s*.png` cut in two) sliding apart; `follow(open_target: float, dt: float)` eases to 0 (shut) or 1 (open). The vehicle view computes `open_target` (1 while a car or a person on a trip is within 6 tiles of the gate line and going through it, else 0). While a car is in the opening (y 14.5 to 21) it is drawn **under** the catwalk bridge (z below the bridge's, above the ground) so it passes beneath the walkway; the deck stays walkable end to end for people (the bridge).
