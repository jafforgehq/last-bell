# Round 10: seasons and the long campaign

> The user's direction (2026-10-02): seasons and a long game. Part of `docs/superpowers/roadmap.md`: four **seasons** with their own rules (length of day and night, weather, how well the garden grows, the cold, how hard travel is), the 30-night game stays as the **Autumn** scenario, a **campaign** of several seasons with a Big Night at the end of each is the long game; tints and sound for each season.

**Goal:** a new game offers two lengths: *Autumn* (the 30-night game exactly as it is now) and the *Campaign* (Autumn, Winter, Spring, Summer in turn, each with its own rules and its own Big Night; surviving the last season's Big Night wins). The settlement game is the one built for it (the small start, the board, the lots) but both starts can play either length.

## Design

**Data** (`assets/data/seasons.json`): per season `id`, `name`, `days`, `blurb`, `dusk` and `dawn` (the hours the dusk begins and the day begins: Autumn 19 and 6.5), `garden` (the beds' yield), `food` and `water` (how much a person uses), `travel` (the road's hours on the board), `waves` (the night's size), `rain` (the chance of rain on a day) and `snow` (the share of those days that are snow), `tint` (the colour the light is warmed or cooled by), `temperature` (the day's mean and swing, for the HUD), `big` (the Big Night's factor over the day's budget).

**The module** (`scripts/sim/seasons.gd`, `sim.seasons`; owner Claude): `campaign` (set before `start_run`), `index`, `current()`, `season_day()`, `end_day()` (the last day of this season: the Big Night is that night), `effective_day(day)` (the day the nights are sized by: the day in the season plus six for each season gone), `mult(key)`, the schedule (`dusk_hour()`, `dawn_hour()`, `phase_at(minutes)`, `light_hour(hour)` which maps the real hour to the standard one so the existing lighting keyframes work), `on_new_day()` (the season turns at the dawn after its last night, the banner says so), `on_dusk()` (the next morning's dawn hour is the season's). In a game that is not a campaign it is Autumn for ever and every answer is the old one.
- Hooked in: `sim` (phase, the bell hour), `scenario` (`total_days()` is the season's last day; victory comes after the last season), `waves` (`budget_for` uses `effective_day` and the season's `waves`, `is_big`, the Big Night's factor), `supplies` (rain and snow chances, the garden, food and water use), `scavenge`/`town` (the road's hours), `tactics` (the forecast hour is two hours before dusk), the storyteller (no card on a season's last day, which it does already through `total_days()`).

**The view** (owner VIEW): the new-game menu (Autumn / Campaign), the HUD's day label ("WINTER, DAY 5 OF 24"), the clock's dusk and dawn marks and the time-to-dusk text from the season's hours, the lighting's tint and light-hour warp, snow (the rain particles made slow, white and wide), the temperature label, the game-over text, the season banner and a hint, README, help list.

**Harness** (owner Claude): `--campaign` plays the long game (`--days=102`), the summary shows the season reached.
