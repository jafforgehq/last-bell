# Round 14: playable trips (take control of the squad)

> The user's idea (2026-10-02): "when we are scavenging, maybe directly control the squad that we send? It would be really cool." Part of docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-02-rounds-11-14.md. Rounds 11 to 13 made the game simpler, the streets alive, and gave trips vehicles and fuel; this makes a trip something you play.

**Goal:** on the Town Board a party has two ways to go: **Auto** (the dice, as now: the harness plays only this) and **Take control** (new). Taking control opens the place as a small map of its own: your squad arrives (at the road's edge or from its vehicle), the dead are about, the place has things to search (shelves, crates, cabinets, fridges, lockers, trunks, pumps) that hold what the place is known for (its loot table), and you move the squad and shoot as you do in the yard (select, right-click to move, the squad fights on its own), search containers by standing at them for a few seconds, and **get out**: the squad's loot, wounds and losses become the trip's result exactly as the dice would have made them. Noise draws the dead. The colony waits while you play (the trip's travel hours still pass for the colony as before: the party is away for the hours the road takes; the raid is played at the moment of sending).

## The contract between the parts

**A site** is what the generator makes for a place and the run plays (a plain Dictionary, `SiteGen.make(place: Dictionary, seed: int, squad_size: int, by_vehicle: bool) -> Dictionary`):

```
{
  "name": String, "kind": String (the place's kind), "size": Vector2i (tiles, about 40 to 56 by 28 to 40),
  "data": Dictionary,         # a map in the schema of assets/data/map.json, built on the base data (GameData.load_all()):
                              # W, H, terrain (ids of terrainTypes), solidZ, solidP, objects (props with pieces), walls, trees,
                              # lights, marks; and no segments, no people, spawns [] : so that scripts/world.gd can build it and
                              # scripts/sim/sim.gd can be given it (see RaidRun)
  "entry": Vector2,           # where the squad appears (the road's edge), with "entry_spread": the squad is spread a little round it
  "exit": Rect2,              # the squad is out when every living member stands in this rectangle (usually round the entry)
  "vehicle_spot": Vector2,    # where a vehicle is drawn by the entry (or Vector2(-1, -1))
  "containers": [ {"id": String, "pos": Vector2 (tile centre where a person stands to search it), "at": Vector2 (the prop's centre),
                   "kind": "shelf"|"crate"|"cabinet"|"fridge"|"locker"|"trunk"|"pump"|"counter"|"bin",
                   "loot": {stock: amount} (food, water, scrap, ammo, meds, fuel; the sum over all containers is about the place's loot table: see below),
                   "search_s": float (seconds of standing there to search it: 3 to 9), "room": String} ],
  "dead": [ {"pos": Vector2, "kind": "walker"|"runner"|"brute", "state": "wander"|"still"} ],   # "still" ones stand until they notice
  "rooms": [ {"name": String, "rect": Rect2} ],       # for the dead to be placed by and for the roofs
  "seed": int
}
```

**Loot:** the sum of all containers' loot is, per stock, what the dice would give the place at its best (the upper end of the place's `loot` spans) scaled by `0.8 + 0.1 x danger`, plus its `extra` stocks with their chances rolled once; the squad can only carry so much (`CARRY` each, more with a vehicle's cargo): the rest stays. **Dead:** `8 + 5 x danger` walkers, a quarter of them runners at danger 2, brutes (1 or 2) at danger 3, plus 3 per point of nest strength; a few are placed outside by the entry so that the arrival is not free.

**A run** (`scripts/raid/raid_run.gd`, `RaidRun.new(site, squad_defs, stock_ammo, vehicle)`): a second `Sim` built on the site's data with the squad as its people (copies of the colony's people: skills, traits, weapon, hp), the dead from the site, nothing of the colony's modules running; `update(dt)`, `order_move(person, tile)`, `order_search(person, container_id)`, `select`, `retreat()` (everyone walks to the exit), `noise` (a shot wakes and draws the dead within 12 tiles), `finished() -> bool`, `result() -> Dictionary` in the trip's result format (`loot`, `hurt` (id to "bite"/"scratch"), `lost` (ids), `clear` (bool, when the place was cleared of the dead), `rescue`, `ammo_used`, `beats` (text lines about what happened)).

**The scene** (`scripts/raid/raid_scene.gd`): a modal view over the game: the site's world, the squad and the dead as pawns (scripts/render/pawn_node.gd), a camera that follows and can be panned and zoomed, selection and orders by mouse and keyboard, the squad's cards (portrait, health, ammo, who is searching), the loot tally, a Retreat button, pause (Space), the end card (what came home, who was hurt, who was lost), and Esc asks "Retreat?". It is opened by `main.gd` from the Town Board's "Take control" button and gives the result back to `scavenge.send`.

## Acceptance

- Rules: a run of a generated site can be played to its end headlessly (a scripted squad walks in, searches, retreats) and its result is a valid trip result; the result of a run is applied exactly as a dice result (loot, wounds, losses, a cleared nest, a rescue); a squad with a vehicle carries more and starts at the vehicle; the dice path is unchanged (the harness identical).
- Sites: every kind of place in assets/data/town.json has a layout; every container is reachable from the entry; the dead are placed by the rules above; the same place and seed make the same site.
- Scene: a person can select the squad and give orders with the mouse, search a container and see its progress, retreat, and read the end card; at 700 to 1920 wide with nothing overlapping or under 12 px.
- Suite green; screenshots of a house, a pharmacy and a gas station in play.
