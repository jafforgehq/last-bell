## What the owner decided (and what this round does with it)

- The base is run by a community that already has jobs, a radio, ideas, nights. What is missing is **the player's place in it**. Three levers, one goal: **POLICY** (standing choices that set how the base behaves: priorities, four dials), **EXCEPTION** (small trouble the base throws up that the player can fix in person or hand to someone by radio, and one hard choice with a timer and costs), **GOAL** (the convoy at the 30th bell, phase 2). Nothing needs the Jobs page any more; the Jobs page stays as the advanced view.
- A **morning briefing** (about 06:30, day 2 on, never pausing) ranks the base's concerns; the player taps up to **four priorities** and the existing automation uses them as weights; an **evening debrief** says what got done and what slipped. Until the player taps, nothing changes ("jobs stay as they are"; the card offers the two suggested priorities in one tap).
- **Four dials** with real trade-offs: RATIONS, WORK HOURS, SCAVENGER BOLDNESS, STRANGERS AT THE GATE.
- **Problems** (leak, jammed generator, quarrel, cracked wall) are world markers with a timer; fix in person (E) or ask someone by radio; unfixed ones escalate. **Facility bottleneck labels** say what each station waits on. **One dilemma**: a fever outbreak, quarantine or treat.
- Avatar mode, settlement game only (`active()`). With no avatar every hook returns the exact neutral value (`* 1.0`, `+ 0.0`, `[]`, `false`) and the game plays byte for byte as before. **No `sim.rng` draw anywhere in the five modules** (choices are pure functions of the state, ties by id; the one "roll" is a hash, S25.3).
- **Not in this round:** a small fire, power or fuel for the base, a kitchen, inner walls, a timetable (the weariness meter stands for "who sleeps when"), selecting buildings by priority, convoy art.

## What stays (do not touch)

Rounds 17 to 24: the verbs, trust, calls, the nights, ideas (`initiative.gd` keeps its 13 kinds; two one-line guards at 0b, S25.5), titles, the gate, levels, the drive. `jobs.gd`'s rules (`auto_assign`, `fill_empty`, `refill`, seats, posts) and `build.gd`'s rules are not rewritten. **The old pages keep doing their job:** the Jobs page (J) stays the advanced view (one row per person, day job and night post by request, Fill empty seats, the three station sliders); whatever the player sets there is **pinned** (S25.1) and the automation never overrides it; the sliders stay the base values and the priorities' shifts are added on top and shown ("Wall line 70% + 10 = 80%"). The Build page (N) still orders buildings, cancels, demolishes; priorities never order a building (the `build` idea and a briefing line point to it). The modal full pages, Shift+J/N/G, are as they were.

### S25.3 PROBLEMS (`problems.gd`, `assets/data/problems.json`; PROBLEMS-SIM)

A problem is a record `{"id": "p<n>", "kind", "day", "t0" (clock_min), "ttl_h", "at": Vector2, "label", "ids": [people], "seg": id, "stage" 0|1|2, "fixer": id, "state": "idle"|"walking"|"working", "cost": {"scrap": n}}`; saved.
| Kind | Where | While open | Fix (in person: seconds of rules time at the marker, scaled by skill) | At stage 2 (half the time) | Past its time |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `leak` | a rain barrel (`rainbarrel` objects) | water drains 0.6 an hour (stock never under 0) | Building: 6 s, 1 scrap | the barrel is half empty: a call | drain x2, water -8 once; stays open |
| `jam` | the generator by the bench (46.8, 28.2) | `blocks("bench")` (no ammo is made) and `blocks("floodlights")` if built; only when no storyteller blackout is on | Building: 9 s, 1 scrap | a call | wrecked: 3 scrap and double time, mood -2 "No power" once; stays open |
| `quarrel` | the midpoint of a pair | each of the two works at x0.85 (`perf_mult`), mood -3 "Quarrel with Walt", `relations.add(a, b, -0.5, "They are quarrelling")` a look | Leading: 7 s hearing both out, see below | a call | `relations.add(a, b, -6)` (rivals), mood -5 each; stays open |
| `crack` | a non-door compound wall section | `sim._damage_segment` 1.5% of its max an hour (floor 25%) | Building: 8 s, 2 scrap (the hp is still mended by the crew or by E as ever; the crack only stops widening) | a call | -20% of its max once; stays open |
