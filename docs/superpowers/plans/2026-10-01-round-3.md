# Round 3: the shell, the look and the workshop

> Follows [round 2](2026-10-01-round-2.md) and the [audit](../../audit/2026-10-01-full-audit.md) (its appendix C is the UI and tutorial blueprint). The user said "yes" to: the workshop, the title screen and menus, the noticeboard re-skin and the tutorial.

**Goal:** make Thirty Bells a game a stranger can start, learn and win. A title screen and menus (so there is a way in, out and back), Orientation Day (so a new player learns the rules by doing), the schoolyard-noticeboard look for the HUD and the menus (so it looks like a product), and a workshop that turns scrap into ammo (so a Standard run can be won, which round 2 showed it cannot: the best crew runs out of ammo around day 24).

**Branch:** `feature/colony-sandbox`. Nothing is pushed or published. **The world art stays exactly as it is** (fences, tiles, props, platforms, the pawns and the dead); only the HUD, the menus and new screens get the noticeboard look. The concept boards in `docs/audit/img/` are references, not targets to copy.

## Global constraints (every builder)

- Godot 4.7.2, GDScript. The suite (507 tests at the start) stays green; new behaviour gets a test that failed first. Run the suite with `/Applications/Godot.app/Contents/MacOS/Godot --headless --path . --script res://tests/run.gd [-- --only=text]`; screenshots with `perl -e 'alarm 90; exec @ARGV' GODOT --path . -- --shot=out.png --frames=40 [--seed= --hour= --advance= --cam=x,y,zoom --size=WxH --fields --lure=N --wall=N --select=id --new=D]` (never run a dev server; headless cannot render). Put scratch files in the session scratchpad, not in the repo.
- **Do not commit.** The owner commits. Do not push, never touch `main`.
- **Never kill processes by pattern** (`pkill`, `killall`): the user keeps the Godot editor open. **Delete only by moving to `~/.Trash`**, never `rm`. Do not delete anything you did not create.
- Shared files (`scripts/main.gd`, `scripts/ui/hud.gd`) are edited by several builders at once: **use Edit (a small string replacement) and never Write over them**, re-read the lines just before you edit, keep your changes in your own region, and do not reformat or reorder what you did not write.
- The HUD helpers `hud._style / _panel / _label / _button / _icon` keep their signatures (optional parameters may be added); the dialogs and the new menus use them.
- Text sizes: nothing under 12 px (`Fonts.MIN_PX`); text on panels reads at 4.5:1 or better (tests/test_hud_text.gd computes it); keep the colour-independent cues the HUD has.
- No franchise names in shipped content. AI-made pictures (Meshy) are logged in `docs/assets-log.md` (what, prompt, model, date, task id, credits) for the rights audit; report the credits you spend. Meshy is pre-approved for this project (the balance was 1,989 credits at the start).
- No downloads from the internet (fonts, packages, images). Barlow and the files already in the repo are the toolbox, plus Meshy and Python (Pillow, NumPy) for pictures.
- Short, honest final report: what you built, files changed, test counts, what you did not do, what the owner should look at.

## Design

| Piece | Rules |
|---|---|
| **The workshop** (`scripts/sim/workshop.gd`, owner) | The yard's worktable (map object 118 at 43.9, 25.7) is the bench; a person stands at `data.workshop` (43.9, 27.1). Key M with a person selected sends them to **make ammo**: each scrap makes 8 ammo; they work 2 + Building/2 scrap an hour (Dez 6.5, Ruth 3.5, Marcus 2.5), slower when hungry or hurt (`sim.perf`); they stop when scrap is down to 3 (kept for walls), at the bell, when drafted or when something takes them away, and an alert says so. The order is toggled by the same key. By day only. |
| **A fourth site** (owner data; ART picture) | `The scrapyard`: danger 3, scrap 35 to 60, no ammo, a little food. Scrap is what the workshop and the walls both eat; the hardware store keeps its ammo. |
| **Title screen** (SHELL) | A cold start (no `--new`, `--seed`, `--load`, `--shot`, `--tutorial` and no `Engine` meta `run`) shows the title over the living yard at dusk (the real world, lamps and fires on, the camera drifting slowly, the HUD hidden), with the **logo** (THIRTY BELLS, drawn from the fonts, a brass bell) and a chalkboard menu: Continue (newest of autosave and quicksave, with its day), New game, Orientation Day (highlighted until it is done), Load, Settings, Credits, Quit (not on the web). New game asks for a difficulty (Story, Standard, Brutal, each with a one-line description) and a seed (random, or typed). A run is started by reloading the scene with `Engine.set_meta("run", {...})` as today. |
| **Pause menu** (SHELL) | Esc with nothing selected (Esc with a selection still clears it) opens it: Resume, Save (quicksave), Load, Settings, How to play (the help panel), Main menu, Quit. The rules pause while it is open. |
| **Settings** (SHELL) | Master, effects, ambience and interface volume (audio buses `SFX`, `UI`, `Ambience`, `Music` under `Master`), UI size (80% to 150%), hints on or off, reduce motion, fullscreen. Kept in `user://prefs.cfg` (`scripts/prefs.gd`), applied at start and when changed. |
| **Saves** (SHELL) | Continue and Load show the autosave and the quicksave with day, time and difficulty read from the file (`SaveGame.peek(path)`); a damaged file says so instead of loading. |
| **Orientation Day** (TUTORIAL) | A scripted scenario (`scripts/sim/orientation.gd`, a scenario module that cannot be lost) taught by a coach overlay (`scripts/ui/coach.gd`) from `assets/data/tutorial.json`: about 14 steps, each with a short text, something highlighted and a done-condition read from the game; the rules pause while a card is shown; skip anywhere; replay from the menu; offered on the first launch (`Prefs` flag `tutorial_done`). It teaches: look around, select, move, release, posts on the decks, repair, the stores, a scavenging trip, the bell, shooting costs ammo, the alarm, a wound, an infection, saving, making ammo. Ends by offering a real run. |
| **The noticeboard look** (ART frames, PORT port) | Slate-green chalkboard panels in oak trim, chalk ink, brass for the clock, hall-pass badges for the crew, paper slips for alerts, notebook paper for modals. Same layout and sizes as now (a look, not a re-layout). The frames are 9-slice PNGs in `assets/ui/theme/` made by `tools/make_ui_theme.py` from Meshy material pictures; `scripts/ui/frames.gd` loads them; where a frame is missing the flat style of today is used. |
| **Report card** (PORT) | The game-over and victory screen restyled as a report card (the same facts), with a "Main menu" button next to New game and Load. |
| **Harness** (owner) | The default policy makes ammo by day when it is low and scrap allows, and picks the scrapyard when scrap is the need. |

## Interfaces (so the builders can work apart)

**Workshop** (owner; PORT and TUTORIAL code against it, with a fake in `tests/hud_kit.gd` until it lands in a few minutes): `sim.workshop` is a module. `crafting(p) -> bool`; `order_craft(p) -> bool`; `stop(p)`; `why_not_craft(p) -> String` ("" when it can start: "it is night", "no scrap", "they are away", ...); `bench() -> Vector2`; constants `AMMO_PER_SCRAP`, `RESERVE_SCRAP`, `scrap_per_hour(p) -> float`. Gizmo id `craft` through `sim.handle_gizmo("craft", sel)` for a person (toggles). `sim.status_of(p)` carries a mark `{"icon": "ammo", "tip": "Making ammo"}` while they work. Alerts `workshop-idle`. Event `{"type": "craft", "x", "y"}` (play the hammer sound).

**Frames** (ART; PORT uses): `scripts/ui/frames.gd` is a static helper: `Frames.box(name: String, tint: Color = Color.WHITE) -> StyleBox` returns a `StyleBoxTexture` with the 9-slice margins of `assets/ui/theme/frames.json`, or `null` when there is no such frame (the caller falls back to its flat style). `Frames.has(name) -> bool`. Frame names, all optional for the caller: `panel` (HUD panel, any size), `panel_inset` (a nested block), `card` and `card_selected` (the crew card, hall-pass badge), `slip` (an alert, tinted by level; paper-coloured), `paper` (a modal page), `tooltip`, `toast`, `chip` (a key chip), `button`, `button_hover`, `button_pressed`, `button_off`, `button_primary`, `button_primary_hover`, `bar_track`, `pill`, `clock` (the brass-rimmed time panel), `banner`. ART may add names; it documents them in `frames.json` (`"name": {"file", "margin": [l, t, r, b], "content": [l, t, r, b]}`).

**Prefs** (owner, `scripts/prefs.gd`, ready before the builders start): static `Prefs.get_value(section, key, default)`, `Prefs.set_value(section, key, value)` (saves at once), `Prefs.flag(name) -> bool`, `Prefs.set_flag(name, value)`; `Prefs.number(section, key) -> float`, `Prefs.on(section, key) -> bool`; `Prefs.path` can be pointed elsewhere by tests (`Prefs.forget()` drops the copy in memory; never name a static function `reload`, it is a built-in method of every script). Sections `audio` (`master`, `sfx`, `ambience`, `ui`, `music`: 0 to 1), `ui` (`scale`, `hints`, `reduce_motion`, `fullscreen`), `flags` (`tutorial_done`, `tutorial_offered`).

**Main** (SHELL and TUTORIAL both edit `scripts/main.gd`): `start_run(difficulty: int, seed: int)` and `load_run(path: String)` reload the scene with the choice in `Engine` meta (SHELL); `start_tutorial()` (TUTORIAL) starts Orientation Day (reload with meta `{"tutorial": true}`); `open_pause()`, `show_title()` (SHELL). `hud.on_menu` is a callback the report card's Main menu button calls (PORT adds it, SHELL sets it). A scenario other than the default is chosen in `_ready` from `run.tutorial`.

**Coach hooks** (TUTORIAL; the owner adds nothing): `hud.anchor_rect(name: String) -> Rect2` (global rect of a named part of the HUD or an empty Rect2): `stores.<ammo|scrap|food|water|meds>`, `card:<person id>`, `clock`, `alerts`, `inspect`, `gizmo:<id>`, `speed.<0..3>`, `fields`, `help`, `dialog.send`; `hud.set_locked(allowed: Callable)` ghosts the buttons that are not allowed; both go in a marked block at the end of `hud.gd` (PORT must leave it). `main._did(kind: String, arg)` is called from the input handlers for what the player does, and `coach.on_event(e)` gets every sim event.

## Acceptance

- **Workshop:** a person at the bench turns scrap into ammo at the stated rates and stops at the reserve, the bell, a draft or a refusal; the stock never goes negative; saves round-trip; the HUD button and tooltip say why when it cannot start. Harness (Standard, 30 days, 16 seeds): the informed policy reaches the victory in some games (a median of at least day 27), the default policy's median stays between 17 and 22, hands-off about 11 to 13.
- **Shell:** a cold start shows the title; New game, Continue, Load, Settings, Credits and Quit work; Esc opens the pause menu; settings persist and apply; a run started with `--new` or `--shot` goes straight to the yard as before; the suite does not need the title.
- **Orientation Day:** a headless bot plays every step through the real handlers to the end; Skip works at every step; the scenario cannot be lost; a first launch offers it.
- **Look:** every HUD panel, the dialogs, the report card and the menus wear the frames; screenshots at 1280x800 and 1920x1080 and at a narrow 980 look right; text contrast tests pass; the world looks as it did.
- **Suite green;** screenshots of the title, the pause menu, settings, the new-game dialog, the HUD by day and by night, a dialog, the report card and three tutorial steps.
