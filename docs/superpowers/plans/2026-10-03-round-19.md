## What the owner decided (and what this round does with it)

- Combat is never one gunner against a horde. The community defends itself every night; an ordinary night is held alone if its posts are manned and its walls decent. The player is a boon on any night and a necessity on two things only: **a Big Night** (a night sized and shaped so that one wall takes the brunt, with plated brutes) and **a breach** (a section that has fallen and cannot be shut under fire without a leader).
- The player answers the radio call "the north wall is going"; he is not the one who has to hold every night. The forecast and the alarm become tools: read the coming night, stay home on purpose, or leave the place in time.
- "Needs the player" is **mechanical and small**: three rules (a leader's presence, a plated brute's weak point, a hot breach) all keyed to one fact, the avatar standing within `RALLY_R` of the thing (S3.2). Numbers live in one data file, `assets/data/nights.json`, so tuning never edits code.

## What was measured while planning (a scratch probe on a snapshot of the last commit; the tuning phase repeats it with the real harness)

### S3.1 BIG NIGHTS AND THE SIZE OF THE NIGHTS (`waves.gd`, `kinds.gd`, `recruits.gd`, `tactics.gd`, `night_data.gd`, `assets/data/nights.json`: NIGHTS-SIM)

**Which nights.** In avatar mode a night is Big when its day in the season (`seasons.season_day(day)`) is a multiple of `big_every` (10) and not the season's last day (the milestones: nights 10 and 20 of Autumn, and of every season of a campaign), or when it is the season's last night (the final, as today, now with plated brutes). **What makes it big:** (a) its size: the day's budget times the theme's `factor` (the final keeps `seasons.big_factor()`); (b) one side: a single heading, `main_share` 0.85 of the dead from that spawn point (an ordinary night: 0.65 over one or two); (c) special dead: `brutes` and `runners` shares of the budget raised, every brute **plated** (S3.2), brutes placed in the middle of the night (`brute_at` 0.25 to 0.6 of the queue, so they reach the wall before dawn); (d) `cap` 70 alive at once (the final keeps 90).

### S3.2 PRESENCE, PLATING AND BREACHES (`nights.gd`, `sim.gd`, `avatar.gd`, `interact.gd`: BREACH-SIM)

One class `Nights` (`sim.nights`, saved with the modules: only `warned` and the hot-breach alert marks). `active()` = `sim.nights_on and sim.player_id != ""`; every function below returns its neutral value when not active, so each hook is one line in the old code.
```
func rallied(p) -> bool          # active, the avatar is up, at the base (not downed, dead, turned or away), p is someone else and within rally.radius (9) tiles of them
func aim_mult(p) -> float        # 1 + rally.aim (1.15) if rallied(p), else 1.0           -> Sim.aim_factor(p) multiplies by it (the avatar's own shots never get it: rallied(avatar) is false)

func weak_hit(z, from: Vector2, dir: Vector2) -> bool   # the pellet's ray passes within weak_r (0.2) x z.size of the head's centre, (z.x, z.y - head (0.62) x z.size): the body's centre is BODY (0.3) up, as Avatar.trace has it

```
`Zombie.plated` (saved with the zombie, optional in a save); `Sim._fire` calls `npc_weak(z)` once per shot at a plated brute and passes `damage_to(z, dealt, weak)` to `_hurt_zombie` (whose signature is unchanged); `Avatar.trace` returns `"weak": bool` as well, `_shoot` sums `damage_to(z, dmg, weak)` per pellet, `_strike` (melee, a pike, the butt) uses `damage_to(z, dmg, false)`. **Reading of the numbers:** against a plated brute (22 to 30 hp) a defender's carbine does about 0.4 of what it does against a plain one (0.92 x 0.2 + 0.08 x 3.0), so a brute draws the guns (they pick brutes first, `Kinds.PRIORITY`) for 20 s and more while the walkers claw freely; the avatar's aimed head hits do 3.0, so a plated brute is down in about 7 s of his fire. That is what "needs the player" means for a brute: not that it cannot be killed, but that the community alone spends a wall's worth of time on it. A brute on an ordinary night is not plated.

**Breach.** A section that falls (state 3) stays open until it has been mended above 40% (`Sim._segment_state`, as now). Nobody mends at night today and nobody starts to (no new autonomy); what is new: (1) a defender sent to mend a hot breach does nothing unless the avatar is rallying them (`repair_mult`), and (2) **the avatar mends by hand**: `Interact` gets a candidate of kind `"wall"`, label "Mend the Red container (35%)", within `mend.reach` (1.6) tiles of `sim.repair_spot(seg)` of a compound section under 95% or down; `mend_wall(seg_id) -> Dictionary` starts a session like the bench (`working = "wall"`, `mend.seconds` 10, ends on moving, the trigger, a hurt or down avatar, a full section or no scrap), at the rate of any repairer (`(REPAIR_BASE + Building) x perf x traits.mult("repair")` through `supplies.repair_budget`, the section's state follows, `skills.credit(p, "mended", ..)`, `morale.on_repaired`), under fire allowed; events `{"type": "wall_work", "on", "seg", "hp"}`; refused with the reason ("there is no scrap to spare"). Priority on a tie: person, post, bench, vehicle, wall. Net: a section lost on a Big Night is shut again only by the avatar's hands, or by two defenders he stands guard for (call option `close`, S3.3); lost for the night otherwise; the day's wall crew mends it at dawn as ever.

### S3.3 CALLS OF THE NIGHT (`radio.gd`, `night_calls.gd`, `scavenge.gd`: CALLS-SIM)

`radio.gd` creates `radio.night = NightCalls.new()` in `setup`, calls `night.watch()` in `update` after `_watch_for_fever` (avatar mode only), sends unknown kinds to `night.carry_out(e, option_id) -> String` from `_carry_out`, and saves it under `"night"`. What `reinforce` does today: `_reinforce(seg)` orders **every** free person (not the leader, no stance, not at something critical) with `sim.order_move` to the posts nearest the section, by day and by night, until `_release_the_sent` at the day: at night everyone is already posted, so it strips the other walls. The old `attack` call keeps that. The new calls send two.

Effects. `hold`/`watch`: nothing. `reinforce`: `_reinforce_near(seg, calls.reinforce_n)`; result "Reinforcements sent (2)". `fall_back`: the old `_fall_back(seg)`. `coming` (offered only while the avatar is at the base and up): `night.focus = {"seg", "until": clock_min + coming_window_min}`; every defender within rally.radius of the section gets `morale.shock(p, coming_morale, "Help is on the way")`; if the avatar is within rally.radius of the section before `until` each of them gets `radio.add_trust(p, kept_trust, "You came when you said")` (once), at `until` without it `broken_trust` ("You said you were coming"). `close`: the two nearest free defenders get `sim.order_repair(p, seg)`; they work only while the avatar rallies them (`repair_mult`), so the call's result says "Dez and Ana go to mend it. They need you there." and the news line follows when it shuts. `lure`: `sim.tactics.order_lure(seg)` on the section chosen by `best_lure_section(avoid)` (the compound section with the most `coverage_of`, then hp, that is not `avoid`, affordable by `why_not_lure == ""`; the option is left out when none); `arms`: `radio.alarm(true)`. Every option that needs the avatar at the base is left out of a call raised while he is away (a place), and the call then reads as a plain warning with `hold`/`reinforce`/`fall_back`.

### S3.4 THE FORECAST AND THE ALARM AS TOOLS

- **The forecast is read** in three places: (1) **a day ahead** (Big Nights only): at `notice_hour` (12:00) of the day before, if someone is at the Radio desk (`jobs.operator("radio")`), `night.watch` makes the news line `forecast` and a `bignight-next` alert (60 s): "Big Night tomorrow: The Siege. About 70 to 95 from the east side (the second bus, the chain-link). 2 plated brutes after 23:00. Be home by 17:20." from `waves.outlook(sim.day + 1)`; nobody at the desk, no notice (the night still comes). (2) **At 17:00**, by the lookout as now, with the new text, the `night` call (when Big, plated or needs_you) and `tactics.forecast()`'s new keys; nobody on watch: "No forecast", as now. (3) **At dusk**, the banner. The HUD shows the marker (V3.1). The line "ready" (`readiness`) is the community's own prediction: walls decent and every post manned means an ordinary night holds.
- **The alarm** (three tools, each already in the rules, none new): *call to arms* (`radio.alarm(true)`: everyone to their posts now, by day or before the bell, until dawn or "stand down"; works from a place's HUD as in round 17); *the lure* (`tactics.order_lure`, 3 scrap, 3 hours, `LURE_PULL` 40; now every brute obeys it in avatar mode, so the player chooses which wall the plated brute breaks on; reachable from the `night` and `brute` calls and the old selected-section "L"); *the bell* (dusk, as now). What changes is where the player meets them: in the calls above and in the harness bot's decisions, not new buttons.
- **Stay home on purpose, or drive back in time.** `scavenge.night_warning(place_id, vehicle_id := "", site_hours := 1.0) -> Dictionary` (pure; the Map page and `Take control` ask it): {"big": tonight is a Big Night (`waves.big_night(sim.day)` or `outlook`), "home_by": hour of the day the party would be home if it left the place after `site_hours`, "leave_by": the hour it must leave the place, "dusk": `seasons.dusk_hour()`, "makes_it": bool, "text": "Big Night tonight: leave the place by 15:40 (the road home takes 1 h 20), or stay out and the base holds alone"}; round trip = 2 x `road_minutes`, margin `road_margin_min` 30. The rule it explains is the existing one: in the settlement game a party is due only while the phase is day (`Scavenge._is_due`), so a trip that reaches the gate after dusk is out until morning. `NightCalls.road_status() -> Dictionary` (while a played trip is in its place or on the road home): {"on", "dusk": clock_min of the dusk, "road_min", "leave_by": clock_min, "left_min": minutes until it (negative when late), "state": "free"|"soon" (left_min <= road_warn_min 60)|"now" (<= 0)|"late" (< -margin: they cannot make it), "big": tonight is a Big Night, "text"}. At "soon" `night_road` news ("Dusk in 2 hours, and a Big Night with it: leave by 15:40"), at "now" the `night_road` call (home: Head home now | stay: Stay out, the base holds alone; default stay) — `home` sets `night.leave_requested = true`, which the place scene reads (V3.1). Staying out costs nothing but the Big Night's rules without the avatar; the radio logs "You stayed out. The base holds the night alone."

## The harness (HARNESS-BOT; `tools/avatar_bot.gd` and `tools/night_lab.gd` new, `tools/balance.gd` edited)

### H3.1 THE POLICY FIX, THE FLAGS AND THE BOT

**The policy must stop treating the avatar as a worker** (measured: left alone it takes seats it never works): `Policy._free` refuses `p.player`, `_jobs` leaves the player out of `alive`, `_place_the_crew` and the trips skip them. Flags (new, additive): `--avatar=none` (default: as before, no player), `--avatar=idle` (Ruth is the player, hands off, put at `Policy.KEEP_SPOTS[5]`, `(30.5, 55.5)` in the school gym: the community alone), `--avatar=bot` (the bot plays her). Game rows gain `"nights"` (the `NightStats` rows) and the summary prints the night table of H3.3.
```
# avatar_bot.gd

```
**Where it goes.** By day: stands at the gate post; no trips (the policy's dice errands are the community's). From the forecast (17:00) it moves to `stand_for` the first of `waves.target_sections(plan)` (why "forecast"); from the bell on, every `THINK_S` it scores sections with `nights.section_threat` plus 3 for a section named in an open call of its own answering and keeps its station unless another beats it by `SWITCH_GAIN` x + 1; it walks `sim.path_p.find(world.solid_p, ...)` waypoints (re-pathed every second and when it has not moved for 0.5 s) by setting `input_move`; the decks are reached by the ladders the grid already has. **How it fights.** Target: a plated brute (aimed at its head point `(z.x, z.y - head x size)`), then any brute, then the dead in `breach` within 6 tiles of any person, then the dead in `attack` at its station's section, nearest first; it fires only when `sim.avatar.trace(from, ang, reach).z != null` along the jittered aim and after `REACT_S` on a new target, stands still to shoot (walking widens the cone), swings (F) at a dead one within 1.4 tiles, presses R when the magazine is at `MAG_LOW` or less and nothing is within `LULL_R`, mends (E, `interact.use()` on a `"wall"` candidate) the station's section when it is under `MEND_BELOW` or down and no dead is within 3 tiles. **Hurt:** below `HURT_HP` health or bleeding it goes to the nearest bed of `sim.medical.beds` and stays until `SAFE_HP` and the bleeding has stopped. **Calls:** `going` -> `coming` (`reinforce` when away), `brute` -> `coming` if plated else `watch`, `breach` -> `close` and goes to mend, `attack` -> `hold`, `night` -> `hold` (the informed policy already places the crew and sets the lure).
```
class NightStats:   # one row for each night, from dusk to the first step of the day
func row() -> Dictionary   # {"day", "kind", "mode", "size" (queue), "plated", "sections_fell" (rising edges of state 3 on compound sections), "fell": [{"seg", "at_hour"}], "down_at_dawn", "hp_lost" (sum of wall hp the dead took, repairs not counted), "min_wall",
                           #  "died", "downed", "bites", "held": sections_fell == 0 and died == 0, "ready": readiness().holds at 18:30, "avatar": {"present" (0..1: of the steps with a section at threat >= 2, the share the avatar was within rally.radius of the most threatened), "shots", "kills", "hp_min", "downed", "died", "ammo"},
                           #  "calls": {"raised", "answered_by_player", "by_default", "mean_s"}, "brute_s": [seconds from a brute's first claw to its death, by whom]}
```
### H3.2 THE NIGHT LAB

**The night lab** (`tools/night_lab.gd`, flags `--days=3,6,9,10,15,20 --seeds=1..8 --reps=6 --modes=none,idle,bot --crew=5 --jobs=4 --out=`): for each seed it plays a no-avatar informed game to 17:30 of each wanted day (`Balance.play`'s loop, once), takes `SaveGame.to_dict(sim)`, and for every mode and rep loads that state into a fresh sim (`SaveGame.apply`), sets `sim.rng.seed = seed x 1000 + rep`, switches the mode on (`none`: the night as today; `idle`/`bot`: `sim.avatar.set_player("ruth")`, the avatar taken over where she stands, then idle/bot), runs to the first step of the next day and returns the `NightStats` row: the same states, three ways, paired (the informed policy keeps ticking every second in every mode, a fresh one from 17:30; a game that is lost before a wanted day gives no state for it and its cells shrink; on Big Night days the `none` column is today's ordinary night of that day, for reference only). A night costs about 1.5 s at 15 Hz: 8 seeds x 6 reps x 3 modes x 6 days is about 25 minutes of one core, 7 with `--jobs=4`. It prints one line a night and the table (a cell = a day group x a mode, at least 48 nights): held %, sections fallen, died, downed, hp lost, presence, avatar shots and ammo, calls answered, ready-stratified held %.

### H3.3 TARGETS AND THE TUNING PROCEDURE (TUNING, phase 2)

| # | Cell | Target |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | ordinary nights 1 to 9, `idle` | held >= 80% (today without an avatar: 82%); when `ready` held >= 90%; someone downed <= 20%; deaths <= 0.03 a night; the full harness (`--avatar=idle --policy=informed --crew=5`, seeds 1, 9, 21, 40 x 8 games, 10 days): >= 90% reach day 11 |
| 2 | Big Nights 10 and 20, `idle` | held <= 35%; a section fell or someone died in >= 70% of nights; the colony is not wiped (>= 95% of games go on) |
| 3 | Big Nights, `bot` | held >= 80%; sections fallen <= 0.3 a night; deaths <= 0.3; presence >= 0.6; the avatar downed <= 0.25 a night and dead <= 0.05 |
| 4 | ordinary nights, `bot` | held >= `idle` held + 5 points (a boon) and never below it minus 2 (not a necessity: `idle` already meets #1) |
| 5 | no avatar | the golden numbers are equal (T-GOLD) |
| 6 | forecast | `first_at` within 1.5 h of the first of the dead at the target sections in >= 80% of lab nights; a target section is among the three with the most hp lost in >= 70% of Big Nights; a lone plated brute under the community's fire lives >= 2.5 x as long as a plain one, and under the bot's <= 10 s from its first claw |

Procedure (each step: change one number in `nights.json`, re-run the lab for the cells it touches with the cache of states; at most four rounds, then record the tables in STATUS.md section 6 and leave the rest to the owner's playtest): (1) `ordinary.avatar_weight` (0.5), then `ordinary.mult` and `ordinary.growth` (0.10 -> about 0.07 if nights 7 to 9 still fall), until #1; (2) the themes' `factor` (1.45, 1.55) until #2; (3) `rally.aim`, `rally.fire`, `rally.radius` until #3 holds with presence 0.6 to 0.8 and a bot that only reaches presence 0.25 gains about +20 points over `idle` (the player must not be a god); (4) `plated.armour`, `weak_mult`, `npc_weak` until #6's brute lines; (5) the calls' ttl and trust numbers last (they hardly move the table). Three things are tuned only with the owner's playtest and said so in STATUS: how a human's presence compares with the bot's, the avatar-mode economy (the harness has no played trips, so its stores are poorer than a played game's: a Big Night held in the lab is held in the game, not the reverse), and the feel of the call timers.

## Risks

1. **The numbers cannot be known from a plan.** Everything tunable is in one file with a measuring instrument (the lab) and a stop rule; the plan fixes the mechanisms and the targets, not the constants.
2. **Today's nights are not held even without an avatar** (measured: sections fall in 18% of nights 1 to 9). "Held alone" is therefore set as "no worse than today, and 90% when ready"; if the owner wants a stricter bar the sizes come down further (`ordinary.mult`), not the rules.
3. **The bot stands in for a human.** It must not be an aimbot (jitter, reaction time, no lead) nor a statue; its presence share is reported so the owner can compare it with his own play. Human tuning is the last word.
4. **Weak point legibility.** A head disc on a top-down sprite can feel arbitrary; the ring on the brute's head (V3.1) and the absence of any new state (no stagger, no phase) keep it simple; if playtest says "unfair" the first fix is a larger `weak_r`, then a lower `armour`.
5. **A refactor of `plan_for`.** `_make_plan` must reproduce the old draws exactly (T-GOLD; the `plan_for` results of 30 days equal before and after).
6. **Call noise.** A Big Night can raise `attack`, `going`, `brute` and `breach` for one wall: `going` settles `attack`, one call a section a night each; the card shows the oldest; ttls are short on purpose.
7. **Two writers near `sim.gd`.** Only BREACH-SIM writes it; `tactics.gd` only NIGHTS-SIM; `radio.gd` only CALLS-SIM; the stubs of phase 0 are the seams.
8. **The harness economy.** Avatar-mode games have errands' penalties and no played trips; the lab avoids it by building states without an avatar and flipping at 17:30; the full `--avatar` games carry the penalty and are labelled so.
9. **Not in this round:** autonomous night mending by the community, mutual aid between posts, relationships and initiative (slice 4), the live drive (slice 5), a new button for anything (the calls and the existing alarm are the interface).
