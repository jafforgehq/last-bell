## What the owner decided (and what this round does with it)

- Places stay generated scenes; **between them is a live drive or walk where things happen** (an ambush, a roadblock, someone flagging you down). It replaces the full-screen overlay (`Sim.fast_forward`) for every trip the player leads in avatar mode. Delegated errands (dice, no scene) and the old game are untouched.
- The base keeps running while you are out: **1 colony step per 3 scene steps at the normal pace**, faster on long roads (the pace rule below); the radio calls, the call card and the News are on screen as in a place.
- The road is **one scene, one road, two legs**: the way there ends at the place's mouth, the place is played as today, the way home is the same road (same ground, new dead, new events). A trip is a drive, a place, a drive.
- The car is **yours to drive, not a minigame**: arcade physics, the dead can be run over, wrecks cost hp, fuel is the abstract trip's fuel, the squad shoots out of the windows. If you do not want to drive, Tab hands the wheel to the best driver (autopilot); it is slower and takes the safe option at every stop.
- Trips on foot get the same road at walking pace with the same events (no car, so no breakdowns, rams or fuel).

## What stays (do not touch)

- `raid_run.gd`, `raid_sim.gd`, `squad_ai.gd`, `site_canvas.gd`, the place's own play, its HUD and report; the old overlay path (`PlaceFlow.road_step`, `RaidHud.show_road`) stays as the fallback for the classic game, the tests and `--fastroad`; `convoy.gd` and the yard's car animations (the party drives in at the gate when the colony brings it home, as now); the harness; the dice (`scavenge._decide*`). ## Controls on the road (avatar mode)

In the car: **W** accelerate, **S** brake then reverse, **A/D** steer, the mouse aims from the driver's window and the left button held fires (spread x1.6 while the car moves), **R** reload, **E** stop at what is ahead (or get back in after a stop), **1 / 2 / 3** the options of an open card (also clickable), **Tab** autopilot on and off (any of WASD takes the wheel back), **Space** pause, **Esc** the menu, **T** News, the Alarm button as ever (the base can be called to arms from the road). On foot (a foot trip, a wrecked car, a stop that gets you out): WASD walk, mouse aim, fire, R, F, E as in a place. The squad's radio verbs are hidden while aboard (the contacts at the base stay).

## The pace and the clock (the rule)

```
# place_flow.gd (static helpers)
const LEG_MIN_S := 30.0; const LEG_MAX_S := 90.0; const MAX_STRIP := 700; const TOP_FACTOR := 1.5
static func leg_seconds(road_min: float) -> float      # clampf(road_min, LEG_MIN_S, LEG_MAX_S): the real seconds a leg takes AT CRUISE
static func pace_for(road_min: float) -> float         # game minutes per real second = road_min / leg_seconds(road_min)
static func cruise_of(kind: Dictionary) -> float       # Avatar.WALK_SPEED (3.2) x kind.speed tiles/s: sedan 7.7, pickup/van 7.0, bus 5.8; on foot {} -> 3.2
static func strip_len(road_min: float, cruise: float) -> int   # mini(roundi(leg_seconds x cruise), MAX_STRIP) tiles
```
`road_min` is `scavenge.road_minutes(place, vehicle)` (the abstract leg: half the round trip by vehicle, the whole way on foot). So an 80-minute road is 80 s at cruise (pace 1.0: one game minute a real second, exactly the place's 1:3), a 36-minute pickup run 30 s (pace 1.2), a 185-minute run 90 s (pace 2.1), the longest walk (the bunker, about 400 min) 90 s (pace 4.4). The colony takes `floor(scene_steps x pace / 3)` steps in all (the place's running-total method, so no remainder is lost). **The clock is the bonus:** the minutes a leg takes are the colony steps it took x 0.1 (a step is 1/30 s at `min_per_sec` 3); `saved_min = road_min - elapsed_min`. Top speed is 1.5 x cruise, so a perfect run arrives in two thirds of the time (up to a third of the road's minutes saved: more daylight at the place and home); slow driving, crashes, grass and stops take minutes (a 12 s stop at pace 2 is 24 game minutes). Fuel is **not** time: `vehicles.take` already burnt `fuel_for` (both legs) at the gate; the gauge shows that trip tank draining by DISTANCE, `fuel_per_leg x progress` with `fuel_per_leg = fuel_for / 2 = burn x road_min / 60`, so an eventless drive there and back burns exactly the abstract trip's fuel and a detour or a stop burns nothing more. Only events move fuel (a stash adds to the base tank, a toll or a leak takes from it, never below 0).

### S21.2 ROAD-GEN (`scripts/drive/road_gen.gd`, `assets/data/road_layouts.json`, `tools/road_preview.gd`)

```
static func make(place: Dictionary, seed: int, leg: String, ctx: Dictionary) -> Dictionary
    # ctx {"kind": vehicle kind id or "", "road_min", "cruise", "len" (strip_len), "plan": RoadEvents.plan(..), "danger", "hour"}
    # returns a SITE in site_gen.gd's shape (so `World.build(site.data)` and `RaidRun` take it as they take a place): name "The road to The pharmacy", kind "road", size (30, len + 24),
    #   data (via SiteGen.map_data), entry (the leg's start, in the middle of the carriageway, 6 tiles in), exit (Rect2: the leg's last 6 rows, the whole width), vehicle_spot (-1, -1), containers [], dead [stragglers], rooms [], seed, danger, and
    #   "road": {"len": float, "width": 30, "style": "urban"|"rural"|"industrial", "start": Vector2, "end": Vector2, "dir": -1 (north: the way there) | +1 (south: the way home),
    #            "centerline": PackedVector2Array, "path": PackedVector2Array (a CAR-WIDE passable line start to end, the proof and the autopilot's line),
    #            "slots": [{"uid", "id", "anchor": Vector2, "y": float, "gap": Rect2 or null, "spawn": Vector2 (dead ahead), "figure": Vector2 or null}],
    #            "dyn": [{"id","file","pos","rot","size","tiles": [grid indices],"ram_hp": float}]   # the roadblock's wrecks: solid, drawn by the scene, removable
    #            "ambient": RoadEvents.ambient_for(..), "leg": "there"|"home"}
```
The same (place, seed) gives the same ground for both legs (`leg` only sets `dir`, `start`, `end`, `entry`, `exit` and the dead and slots of that leg's plan). Method: the carriageway is 8 wide (two lanes) with a 2-wide verge, 30 tiles across; its centre follows `x(y) = base + 3.2 sin(2 pi y / 140 + phi) + 1.6 sin(2 pi y / 57 + psi)` (|dx/dy| <= 0.25: straight to gently winding; the foot strip is the same road); rows are painted ASPHALT/SIDEWALK/GRASS/DIRT with `Canvas.fill`, `lanes` marks every 16 rows, `trees`, `scatter` of shrub/hydrant/lamppost/trashbags/barrel/tires, `decals` (blood, stain, crack, papers), `cars` (`car_h`, `car_v`, `car_police`, `van_h`, `pickup_v`, `bus_h`) on the verges and shoulders only (never inside the car-wide path), `stamp` of the `site_layouts.json` templates (`house_a`, `house_b`, `terrace_house`...) 10-24 tiles off the road for "urban", fields and fences for "rural" (place kinds farm, water_works, checkpoint), containers and barrels for "industrial" (scrapyard, warehouse, depot, power_station...); the style is by `place.kind`. Wrecks scale with danger (3 + 3 x danger per 100 tiles of road). Each plan entry reserves a slot of the road at its `at` and gets its props: **block** a staggered line of cars across the carriageway with one gap of 3.4 (car) / 1.4 (foot), the wrecks as `dyn`; **stop** a figure or props at the verge (a person at `figure` for a survivor, a tanker for fuel, a barrier and two figures for a checkpoint); **dead** an empty reserved stretch (the scene spawns the dead into `spawn`); **forced** nothing. The dead of the strip are `2 + 2 x danger` for each 100 tiles, "still" or "wander", none within 20 tiles of the start. Budgets for the web: at most 20 000 tiles and 350 objects. `RoadGen.passable(site, width) -> bool` (BFS on a grid whose cells are clear of solids within `width / 2 + 0.4` of their middle, 8 neighbours, from `start` to `end`) is the test's proof and runs inside `make` (a strip that fails is regenerated with `seed + 1` up to 5 times, then the wrecks are dropped).

### S21.3 CAR-SIM and AUTOPILOT (`scripts/drive/car_sim.gd`, `scripts/drive/autopilot.gd`; pure, no nodes, fixed step 1/30)

```
class CarSim: ACCEL 5.5 t/s2, BRAKE 13, REVERSE_MAX 2.5, DRAG 0.30 /s, ROLL 0.8 t/s2, TURN_RATE 1.9 rad/s (x clamp(|v|/4, 0, 1), x0.45 at top speed), top = cruise x 1.5
const GROUND := {"ASPHALT": 1.0, "SIDEWALK": 0.9, "DIRT": 0.75, "GRASS": 0.6, "SOIL": 0.5}     # the share of top speed on that ground (the car slows toward it)
const SOFT_IMPACT 2.0 (t/s), IMPACT_HP 1.4 per t/s above it, BOUNCE 0.35 (speed kept in a frontal crash), RAM_SPEED 6.0, RAM_KEEP 0.55, RAM_HP 8 (a car wreck) / 15 (a bus), RUN_OVER_SPEED 4.0, RUN_OVER_HP 0.35, BRUTE_HP 6.0 (speed x0.6, it is not killed), CLAW_DPS 0.8 (each dead one in contact below 2 t/s, at most 6)
var pos: Vector2; var heading: float (0 = east, -PI/2 = north); var speed: float; var hp: float; var kind: Dictionary; var cruise: float; var top: float
func setup(kind: Dictionary, start: Vector2, heading: float, hp: float) -> void
func step(dt: float, throttle: float, steer: float, probe: Callable) -> Dictionary   # probe(Vector2) -> {"solid": bool, "ground": String, "dyn": String}; returns {"hit": impact, "hp_lost": n, "scrape": bool, "ram": [dyn ids], "wrecked": bool}
func circles() -> Array         # three circles of radius w/2 along the heading: the body for the collision
func progress(road: Dictionary) -> float      # distance along the centerline from `start` over `len`, 0..1
func fuel_burnt(per_leg: float, progress: float) -> float    # per_leg x progress
func footprint() -> PackedVector2Array     # the rotated rectangle (for zombies' push-out and the drawing)
class Autopilot: static func drive(car, road: Dictionary, dt: float, aware: Dictionary) -> Dictionary   # {"throttle","steer"}: pure pursuit on road.path with a look-ahead of max(6, 0.8 x speed); target speed 0.8 x cruise, down to 2.5 t/s when a solid or a zombie stands in the 3-wide corridor within 14 tiles; stops for an open stop's card
```
Collisions resolve against the grid's solid tiles and `dyn`: the car is pushed out along the contact normal, the normal speed is lost (`BOUNCE`), hp falls by `(impact - SOFT_IMPACT) x IMPACT_HP`; a `dyn` wreck hit at >= RAM_SPEED is knocked aside (its tiles open, the scene slides and turns its picture) at RAM_HP and RAM_KEEP, below that it is a wall. hp 0 is a wreck: the car stops, the squad steps out, the vehicle is `wreck`ed, the rest of the road is on foot at 3.2 t/s (so: minutes lost).

### S21.4 FLOW and the hand-off (`place_flow.gd`; `main.gd` `open_raid` and the flags)

The hand-off, in order: (1) `main.open_raid` as today (`pick_party`, `flow.begin`), then `flow.live = PlaceFlow.live_for(sim)`; live: `DriveScene.new()`, `scene.setup_trip(...)` with a `make_place_site` that is today's `maker.make(place, seed_value, size, by_vehicle)`; not live: today's code. `on_done` and `finished` are unchanged. (2) The party is "in_place" from the gate (colony people gone, the car out, both legs' fuel burnt). (3) The leg there ends at the road's end (or the squad is wiped: `failed`): `flow.end_leg`; then the place's site is made, `_swap_stage` rebuilds world, run, pawns and camera for it (a one-frame card hides the hitch), `flow.arrive()`, banner "At the pharmacy"; the place is played by the base's own code; a wrecked car means `vehicle_kind ""` at the place. (4) The place over: `final_result = flow.merge_road(run.result())`, `flow.finish(final_result)` (trust, the leader's succession and `_agree_on_the_leader` as today); nobody coming home: straight to the report; else the same road is rebuilt as the home leg (`leg "home"`, `dir` south, new plan with `leg: "home"`, the dead of that leg), `flow.begin_leg("home")`. (5) The leg home ends: `flow.end_leg`, `flow.home()`, the base's `_come_home()` (report over the colony's view, hold). The party then drives in at the gate by the colony's own routes and unloads, the vehicle parks in its bay worn by the road's hp and the place's danger (`vehicles.come_home`): that is how the vehicle ends parked. (6) A trip that crosses dusk arrives at night: `night_ok` lets the party in; the night road's headlights and dead are the dread. (7) The avatar dies on the road: the run's own succession (`on_avatar_down`) hands the lead to the next aboard, the banner says who leads, the base is told at the end of the trip by `_agree_on_the_leader` (loss, setback and news if she is lost); all down on the road: `failed`, no place, no road home, the colony settles the party (`scavenge._finish_party` with nobody coming). (8) The base falls while out: `_on_place_over` ends the leg as the base class ends a place. (9) A save on the road loads as a place does: the party is "in_place" and turns back with nothing, or "away" and arrives by the abstract clock (`road_live` is not saved).

Staging: `--drive=<place id or kind>[,<seed>[,<vehicle kind>]]` (as `--raid`: the first three of the crew, the vehicle put in the fleet and fuelled), `--drive-do=<steps joined by +>`: `at:<share>` (the car put there), `speed:<share of cruise>`, `event:<id>@<share>` (the plan is forced), `hour:<h>`, `wait:<s>`, `aim:<x>:<y>`, `fire:<s>`, `autopilot`, `crash`, `stop`, `leg:home` (skips the place), `end`, plus the base's `news`, `alarm`, `down`; `--noevents`; `--fastroad`. `main._stage_drive(spec)` is `_stage_raid`'s twin and the scene's `stage` gets the new steps. ## Risks

1. **The subclass of `RaidScene`.** It leans on 1600 lines it does not own. Keeping `phase` "place" avoids every gate; the overrides are the list in V21.1 and nothing else; a base change to one of them breaks `test_drive_scene.gd` first, on purpose. Fallback if it proves brittle: the overlay path is intact behind `live_enabled`.
2. **Web performance.** One 30 x 700 strip is 20 000 tiles and at most 350 objects, built once per leg behind a one-frame card; a leg's colony stepping is at most about 1.5 colony steps per scene step (pace 4.4, the longest walk); the dead are 2 + 2 x danger per 100 tiles and sleep until noticed; no per-step path search for the aboard squad (`squad_goal` pins them). Measure the build and a 60 s drive in the web build before the film; if the hitch exceeds a second, lower `MAX_STRIP` (the leg gets slower pace, not a shorter time).
3. **The 1:3 colony stepping on long roads.** A leg is 30 to 90 real seconds whatever the road: the pace rises (to about 4.4) rather than the time; the call card's countdown reads at that pace (`speed_getter` returns `flow.leg_pace / 3`).
4. **Arrive early as a bonus is a clock, not a number.** There is no separate bonus: the colony clock is behind a nominal trip's by `saved_min`. The report and the road log say it in minutes. If it proves too weak or too strong, tune `TOP_FACTOR` and `GROUND`, not the clock.
5. **Balance.** The road adds risk and reward to played trips only; the harness and the dice do not change. The numbers live in constants and in `road_events.json` (weights, counts, effects); tune by playing. A road that kills too often: lower the ambush counts and `CLAW_DPS`; one that is never a threat: raise `weight` of the dead kinds.
6. **Passability is a promise.** The generator proves a car-wide line and regenerates on failure; the autopilot follows the proof; a block always has its gap and a clear option; a car stuck against a wall can reverse, and a stuck car (no progress for 20 s, speed 0) is nudged to the path by the scene with a log line rather than ending the trip.
7. **A trip is now minutes.** Two legs of 30 to 90 s plus the place: a trip is about 3 to 6 real minutes. This is the owner's decision (the base runs while you are out); Tab is the escape for a leg you do not want to play.
8. **Dusk and the gate.** A party that arrives at night comes in (`night_ok`) with no change to the gate, which is still a picture; dead at the gate are not checked on arrival (the dice's rule never did for the return). Opening the gate for real stays for a later round.
9. **Art.** No new art: wrecks, trees, houses, props, terrain and the four vehicle pictures exist; the vehicle pictures are turned, not redrawn (nose from `vehicles.json`); a rammed wreck slides and turns its own picture. The engine loop (`car_engine_loop.wav`, pitched by speed) is optional polish via `tools/make_car_sounds.py` and the headless import; without it the engine start, the crash and the thud already exist.
10. **Not in this round:** the gate opening for real, hijacking or leaving vehicles on the road, convoys of two cars, weather, a road between two places (every road is base to place), outposts' garrison runs, door animations on the road.
