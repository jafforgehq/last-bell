# Round 5: jobs and stations

> The user's direction (2026-10-02): the game should grow from a small crew into a settlement. The order is **jobs and stations** (this round), then **buildings** you can actually build (round 6), then a **small start with recruits who join over the radio** (round 7, the crew starts at three). The itch demo and the store page wait until these are done ("we are not in a rush"). Follows [round 4](2026-10-01-round-4.md) and the audit's appendix B (the jobs idea of the spec appendix 4.5 and the user's wish to "actually assign people").

**Goal:** make management a real activity. Today a day job is a decorative standing spot, the orders (repair, make ammo, auto-repair, post) are one-off and per person, and the night posts are fixed. After this round every person has a **day job** (a station with seats and an effect) and a **night post** (a named place on the wall), you assign them on a **Jobs screen**, the stations do real things (grow food, make ammo, mend walls, treat the hurt, take the noon call, keep watch), an **Auto-assign** button fills them by skill, and the choices you make (who sits at the radio, who is on the wall crew, where the best shooters stand) change how a day and a night go.

**Branch:** `main` (the user asked to work there from now on). Nothing is pushed unless asked. **The world art stays as it is**; the Jobs screen and the markers use the noticeboard frames.

## Design

**Precedence of what a person does** (first that applies): not able (dead, downed, away, breakdown) → nothing; **an order** (right-click, R draft, P to post, F repair one wall) → obey it; **medical duty** (treating someone) and **a scavenging trip** as today; **at night** (from the bell to dawn) → go to the **night post** and hold it; **by day** → work at the **day job's station** if it has work; if it has none, do **chores** (mend a wall under 50%, treat a patient, only if able to), else stand **at ease** at the station's spot. Orders end when released (R again), and then the person goes back to their job.

**Stations** (data: `assets/data/stations.json`; the first version of 6; each has a spot, seats, a skill that makes the work faster, an effect, settings):

| Station | Where | Seats | Skill | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Workbench** | the yard's worktable (43.9, 27.1) | 2 | Building | Makes ammo from scrap (8 ammo a scrap; 2 + Building/2 scrap an hour, as the workshop does today) while the ammo is under the **target** (setting, default 300) and the scrap above the **reserve** (setting, default 3); with nothing to make the worker does chores. |
| **Wall crew** | the walkway by the gate (45.5, 18.8), then any worn wall | 3 | Building | Mends the most worn wall by day while there is scrap, under the **threshold** (setting, default 70%); this is today's auto-repair, as a job. |
| **Vegetable beds** | the garden (53.5, 31.5) | 2 | none | The beds grow food by the hour of work: about 0.2 food an hour for each gardener on top of a small share that grows unattended; Hector alone gives about what the beds give today (3 a day). |
| **Infirmary** | the infirmary (48.5, 53.5) | 2 | Medicine | Treats the hurt and the sick first; while someone is on duty here, patients resting in the beds heal 1.5 times as fast. |
| **Radio desk** | the radio in the hall (60.5, 46.6) | 1 | Leading | The noon call (round 4's radio hour) comes only if someone is at the desk at 12:00, and that person speaks; with nobody there the radio stays silent that day and an alert says so. |
| **Lookout post** | the north-west tower (18.9, 16.9) | 1 | Shooting | The 17:00 forecast comes only from someone on watch; it is that person who reports (today it is "the Lookout", Marcus). |

**Night posts** (data: `assets/data/posts.json`): 19 named places on the decks and towers (the six people's present posts among them): the north walkway (nine along it, the barricade, the gate and the bus among them), the west and east walls (four each; the west fence is Ana's), the two towers. One person to a post. A person's post is kept from night to night (today it is fixed to the person); `P` sends someone to theirs. The fields-of-fire view (V) is how you choose.

**The Jobs screen** (key **J**, a toolbar button; the rules pause while it is open): a table with one row for each person (portrait, name, mood, **day job** and **night post** as buttons that open a list with the seats left, their skills with the station's skill marked, what they are doing now), the stations below or beside it (seats, one line of what they do, their settings as sliders), and the buttons **Auto-assign** (fills the seats by skill and trait) and **Clear**. Hovering a row, a station or a post marks its place in the world. The person panel shows the job ("Job: Workbench"), and the keys **M** (workbench) and **B** (wall crew) toggle that assignment for the selected person; **F** stays a one-off repair order. A critical station left empty says so (`job-radio`, `job-watch` alerts).

## Interfaces (the working first version of `scripts/sim/jobs.gd` carries them)

`sim.jobs` (module):
- `stations() -> Array` of `{"id", "name", "where", "blurb", "pos": Vector2, "seats", "skill", "icon", "settings": {key: {"label", "default", "min", "max", "step", "unit"}}}`; `station(id) -> Dictionary`.
- `job_of(p) -> String` (a station id, or "" for none); `assign(p, station_id) -> bool` (moves them from their old station; false when full or the person cannot); `unassign(p)`; `why_not(p, station_id) -> String` ("" if they can); `crew_of(station_id) -> Array` (assigned, alive); `seats_left(station_id) -> int`; `seat_pos(p) -> Vector2` (where they stand at their station).
- `working(station_id) -> Array`: the assigned people who are **there** and free to work right now (able, by day, no order, not in a trip or a breakdown, within `AT_STATION` of their seat): **the effect modules ask this**. `operator(station_id)` is the first of them or null.
- `setting(station_id, key)`, `set_setting(station_id, key, value)` (clamped to the data's range).
- `posts() -> Array` of `{"id", "name", "pos": Vector2, "face"}`; `post_of(p) -> Dictionary`; `set_post(p, post_id) -> bool` (one person to a post: refused if taken); `post_pos(p) -> Vector2` (replaces reading `def.post`).
- `auto_assign()`; `clear()`; `chores_for(p)` (the fallback described above, used by the goal).
- `goal_for(p)`: the day-job goal for `sim._goal_for` (`{"x", "y", "face", "what"}` or null).
- `status_of(p) -> Array` (a mark such as "At the workbench"), events `{"type": "assigned", "who", "station"}`, alerts `job-radio`, `job-watch`.
- `gizmo("job_bench" | "job_builders", sel)` for the M and B keys (toggle).
Compatibility shims for the effect builder: `workshop.order_craft(p)` and `tactics.auto_repair(p)` keep working but mean "assigned to the workbench / wall crew".

## Acceptance

- **Rules:** a person assigned to a station walks there by day and works; a full station refuses; an order overrides and releasing it sends them back; at the bell everyone goes to their post and the post persists to the next night; a station with nothing to do gives chores; the effects are as in the table (garden by the hour, beds ×1.5 while the infirmary is staffed, the radio and the forecast need a person at the desk and the post); auto-assign fills by skill; saves round-trip and an old save gets the default jobs; the default jobs reproduce today's economy within the harness's noise.
- **Screen:** the Jobs screen works with the mouse and the keyboard at 700, 980, 1280 and 1920 wide; every assignment is possible from it and from the person panel and the M and B keys; hover marks show in the world; Auto-assign and Clear work; the settings sliders change the stations' behaviour.

- **Suite green;** tutorial green (a step teaches the Jobs screen); screenshots of the Jobs screen, the person panel and the world marks.

## Result (2026-10-02)

Built as planned (J1 jobs core and posts, J2 the stations' effects, J3 the Jobs screen, J4 the policy, the tutorial step, the docs). The suite passes (1273 tests). What changed from the plan:

- **Auto-assign** fills the work first (bench, wall crew, beds), then the lookout post, the infirmary and the radio desk, so that a short-handed crew works before it listens (round 7 starts with three people). A crew of five leaves the radio desk empty, a crew of three all three desks.
- **The wall crew's trips end with the scrap** (the person is called back to their station) and a new job (or none) calls a person off the wall the crew sent them to. An order of the player's to repair is still kept until the wall is mended or the scrap arrives.
- **The harness policy** needed three things a player would do: call the crew in at 18:15 and not 17:00 (a station's hours are now worth something), the best builder on the bench, and desks only for the people beyond three hands of work (`DESKS`, `FLEX_HANDS`, `PICK_ORDER`, `PLACE_HOUR` in `tools/balance.gd`). With the first, naive policy the informed wins fell from 6 of 16 to 2 of 16.
- **A fresh review of the whole change set** (a reader who had not written any of it, with fuzz runs of random orders, assignments and saves) found five real defects, all fixed with tests that failed first: the second the wall crew is sent on was not saved (a loaded game sent Walt at another moment than the saved one); a job change could cancel a repair the player had ordered; a section whose mender had turned stayed "taken" and was never mended again; a seat held by someone on a scavenging trip could not be covered (a station now counts such a seat as free to whoever is here, and taking it sends the holder back without a job; Auto-assign puts the people who are here to work first and gives those away what is left); and the workshop's idle alert came again after a load. A forced radio card with nobody up to name no longer shows braces, and a card that needs people counts only the well.
- **No game constant was changed to bring the balance back**: the numbers in the table above (garden 0.2 food an hour and 0.6 a dawn, infirmary heal x1.5, bench target 300 and reserve 3, wall crew threshold 70%) are the first values and stand.

Balance (Standard, 30 days): default median day 18 (16 seeds; round 4 the same), informed 12 wins of 32 (38%; round 4 the same), Story informed 8 of 8, hands-off median 9 (11), Brutal informed mean day 16.9 (17.8). Acceptance met.

Not done: the `{lookout}` token of the radio cards still names Marcus whoever is on watch; the Jobs screen has no keyboard shortcut for a single assignment (the person panel's M and B keys do that); stations cannot be added by building yet (round 6).
