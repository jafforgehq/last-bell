# Rounds 11 to 14: simpler, alive, vehicles, playable trips

> The user's feedback after playing rounds 7 to 10 (2026-10-02): the game is **too complex** right now, with **too many building slots**; the city where the base is feels **dead and empty** (more assets, more zombies, an option to go out); and they want **vehicles and fuel** (fill a car with fuel and send people to another city) and to **directly control the squad** when scavenging. They asked for all four, starting with the cut. Each round below is built the way earlier rounds were (plan, core, builders on disjoint files, an independent review, the harness, a green suite, a commit); round 12 to 14 get their own plan files when they start.

## Round 11: the cut (this file)

**Goal:** fewer things to look at and decide. Nothing is taken away that the game needs; what goes is clutter, and what stays is easier to use.

- **Plots:** the yard keeps **5 yard plots and 2 wall plots** (was 9 and 3); the ring has **6 plots** (was 17): north 3, west 2, east 1. With seven plots in the compound and seven buildings to choose from, the player chooses what to build first; the ring is the room for more.
- **Buildings:** the ring-only orchard and cistern go (the greenhouse and the barrels do their jobs); the scrap yard stays as the one ring-only building. Ten building kinds become eight.
- **Walls at the street mouths are one decision, not twelve:** each claimed lot has a **standing order** ("Wall the streets as the scrap allows", `lots.set_auto_walling(lot, bool)`): the module orders the next open street's wall by itself (the sturdiest kit for its price) when nothing is going up and the scrap is there, one after another. The per-mouth kit picker goes from the screens; a mouth is a pin with its state, and its card says what stands there. The core is written and tested (`scripts/sim/lots.gd`: `auto_walling`, `set_auto_walling`, `next_street`, `next_price`, `best_kit`).
- **The yard stops being covered in signs:** an empty plot's hammer sign and dashed outline are shown only while the Build screen is open, for the plot the mouse is near (about 3 tiles) and for plots the player has a site on, not all the time.
- **Fewer places to click:** the Lots page is the first slip of the Build screen only while a lot can be claimed; the lot cards read: price, what is needed, one button, and once claimed the standing-order switch with "next: the west alley, 11 scrap" and "n of m walls stand".
