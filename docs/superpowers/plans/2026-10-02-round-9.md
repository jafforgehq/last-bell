# Round 9: growing the walls

> The user's direction (2026-10-02): a bigger base to defend as well as a bigger world to explore (round 8 is the world). Part of `docs/superpowers/roadmap.md`. The roadmap's wording is "claim adjacent lots, wall them in, new plots, new posts, new directions the dead come from; about three times the area".

**What the map already has.** The 84 x 64 map is a block: the compound (yard 44 x 26 and the school) sits in a ring of streets (the north street, the west and east roads, sidewalks, parked cars) that is closed by rows of buildings at the map's edge. The ring is open at **ten mouths**, and the ten spawn points of the dead sit on them: the tops and bottoms of the west and east roads (4), the two ends of the north street (2), the two alleys on the north row (2), the west alley (0.5, 37) and the east alley (83.5, 41). The ring has about two and a half times the ground of the yard and the school together. **Round 9 lets the player take the ring**: close the mouths with walls built from scrap, and use the streets as ground to build on. No new map art is needed; the base grows from the yard to the whole block and the defence moves outward, from four sides to ten chokepoints, with the old walls as the second line.

**Goal:** a settlement game (the small start, `sim.town.on`) can **claim lots** of the ring on the Build screen (the board's cleared places and scrap pay for it), which opens the Main gate to people (the dead stay out), adds **ring plots** to build on (the seven buildings of round 6 and more room for them), and adds **wall plots at the mouths** where the Builders put up a wall of the kit of the compound's own walls (containers, buses, cars, pallets, chain-link, cinderblock): each is a wall section like the old ones (hp, damage states, slots for the dead, a post for a shooter). A lot's buildings stand in the open until its mouths are shut: at the bell each building of an unsealed lot risks being raided (like an unguarded outpost, round 8). The classic start (six people, tutorial) is untouched: nothing here exists in it.

## Design

**Data** (`assets/data/lots.json`, owner DATA):
- `lots`: three lots, `north` (the north street, its two ends, the two alleys on the north row, the tops of the west and east roads: six mouths), `west` (the west alley, the bottom of the west road) and `east` (the east alley, the bottom of the east road). Each: `id`, `name`, `blurb`, `claim` {`scrap`, `cleared` (how many board places must be cleared), `day` (earliest)}, `mouths` (ids), `plots` (ids), `sealed_bonus` (a small morale lift when it is sealed).
- `mouths`: each is a wall plot closing a street: `id`, `name`, `lot`, `spawn` (the index of the spawn point it closes), `dir` ("h" a wall running east to west, "v" north to south), `rect` [x0, y0, x1, y1] (the tiles it shuts, whole tiles, three thick), `center`, `length`, `kits` (the kinds that fit), `slots` (the dead's places on the outer face: `x, y, ax, ay, face`, like the old segments), `post` {`pos`, `what`} (where a shooter stands behind it), `outside` and `inside` (a tile each side, to test that a built wall closes the street). A mouth is at least 3 tiles inside the spawn point (the dead spawn outside the wall).
- `plots`: the ring's building plots, in the format of `plots.json` (3 x 3 lots on open ground, `work` spot, reachable on foot from the gate once a lot is claimed) with `kind: "ring"` and a `lot`.
- `kits`: the wall kinds: `id`, `name`, `dir` ("h" or "v"), `length`, `hp`, `scrap`, `work` (builders' hours), `template` (the id of the compound's own segment whose damage-state pictures it moves and scales: container N 0, pallets N 1, bus N 3, car N 4, container W 6, chain-link W 7, cinderblock W 8, pallets W 9, bus E 12 ...). A kit is only offered on a mouth of its `dir`, at most about 15 % scaled along its length to fit.
- `passage`: the id of the old segment that people may walk through once a lot is claimed (the Main gate, 2): its tiles open on the people's grid, never on the dead's.

**The module** (`scripts/sim/lots.gd`, `sim.lots`; owner Claude): `on`, `lots()`, `lot(id)`, `claimed(id)`, `can_claim(id)`/`why_not_claim(id)`, `claim(id)`, `mouths()`, `mouth(id)`, the wall at a mouth (`kit_of(id)`, `built(id)`), ordering and building through the existing site machinery of `build.gd` (a wall plot is a plot with a kit: `build.order(plot_id, kit_id)`, the Builders work on it, on completion a **Segment** is added to `sim.segments` with the kit's hp, its tiles shut on both grids and its slots), the people's passage, `sealed(lot_id)`, the night's raid on the buildings of an unsealed lot, saves.
- **Dynamic segments:** `sim.add_segment(def)` appends a `Segment` (ids from 100) and tells the world (`world.add_segment_art`), the overlay and the tactics; segments are only ever appended, in the order they were built, so a save restores them by replaying the list (indexes stay stable).
- **The dead and the new walls:** `assign_slot` in a lots game only offers a slot the dead one can reach (a flood fill of the dead's grid, redone when a wall goes up or down); the old `yard_open` rule (a down yard wall opens the school doors to the dead) counts the compound's own sixteen sections only. A mouth wall that falls opens the street.
- **Raids:** at the bell each building in a claimed, unsealed lot has `RAID_RISK` (0.10, less with a garrison-like nothing: the lot's mouths built reduce it by their share) of being ruined; a ruined building is demolished with no refund and a banner says so.

**The screens** (owner VIEW): the Build screen (key N) gets the lots: a row of lot cards (claim button with its price and the reason it cannot be claimed yet), the ring plots and the mouths as pins on its map of the compound (the Build screen's map grows to the whole block when a lot is claimed, or has a zoom-out toggle), a mouth's card offering its kits with price, hp and hours. `world.add_segment_art` draws a built wall from its kit's template pieces, the segment's four damage states as the old ones. The wall-health display in the HUD and the overlay's bars cover the new segments. Hints, README, the help list.

**Harness** (owner Claude): the informed policy claims lots when it can, builds the mouth that the forecast names first, then the others; the default policy does not.
