# Round 8: the Town Board

> The user's direction (2026-10-02): a much bigger world. Part of `docs/superpowers/roadmap.md`: **a large town map pinned on the noticeboard**, hidden until scouted; expeditions that travel by distance and danger; cleared places that become outposts; the dead come from nests on the board; radio calls and rescues are where recruits come from. It replaces the scavenging dialog **in the small start** (round 7's `crew_start == 3`, which is now the settlement game); the classic start (six people, Orientation Day) keeps the old four-site dialog exactly as it is.

**Goal:** the world beyond the wall is a place you plan on. A town of about 24 places on a board: houses, a pharmacy, a hardware store, a scrapyard, a church, a farm, a clinic, a police station, a fire station, a garage, a bakery, a warehouse, a bus depot, a radio shop, a bunker... Most are hidden. Parties go out in the morning, travel (the road distance sets the hours), search, and come back the same day or, for the far places, the next. Scouting uncovers neighbours; some places hold **survivors** (a rescue brings a recruit home with the party), some hold **nests** (the nights lean toward them: clear a nest and that direction quietens), a cleared place can be **claimed** as an outpost (a daily yield; an unguarded outpost can be overrun at night, a garrison holds it).

## Design

**The board.** `assets/data/town.json`: `board` {w: 100, h: 70}, `home` (the school's pin), `places` (each: `id`, `name`, `kind`, `pos` [x, y], `blurb`, `danger` 0 to 3, `loot` and `extra` in the format of `sites.json`, `nest` {`strength` 0 to 3}, `survivor` (a caller def key or a generated one: bool), `outpost` {`yield` {stock: a day's amount}, `cost` scrap}, `known` (shown from the start), `image` (optional)), `roads` ([[a, b], ...] between place ids and `home`), `beats` (travel and search events: `id`, `text`, `chance`, `effects`: loot delta, a wound, morale, a delay in hours, a reveal). The four old sites are places (the same loot).

**The module** (`scripts/sim/town.gd`, `sim.town`; owner Claude): the graph and its road distances (`hours_to(id)` = road length / `SPEED` tiles an hour), the **fog** (`known`), `cleared`, `claimed` outposts and their garrison, the nests (`pressure(heading)`, read by `waves.gd`), `survivors` waiting, the outposts' daily yield at dawn and their nightly risk, saves. The expedition machinery stays in `scavenge.gd` and is generalised: in the settlement game `sites` is the list of known places, the party's `return_at` is `depart + 2 x hours_to + SEARCH_H`, a party may be out overnight (it then misses the night's wall and has an extra chance of a wound), `_decide` also rolls the beats (they can reveal places, delay the return, wound, find a cache), and a trip may be **scout** (no loot; reveals the places within `SCOUT_RADIUS`), **clear** (a nest: danger x2, on success the nest is gone), **claim** (a cleared place with an outpost: costs scrap, becomes an outpost) or **garrison** (stay at a claimed outpost until recalled).

**Nests and the nights.** Each nest has a heading (the spawn point nearest to it); `waves.plan_for` weights the main spawn points by `1 + pressure` and the night's budget by `1 + NEST_BUDGET x total strength`; clearing lowers both. The forecast already tells the heading.

**Recruits and rescues.** A place with `survivor` true, once visited and cleared of danger, adds a caller to the front of `sim.recruits.queue` (a rescue) or, if the party was sent to `rescue` it, brings them home with the party (arrival at the gate when the party returns; housing applies). The radio calls name places (a new call kind: "someone is hiding at the church": reveals the place and marks the survivor).

**The Town Board screen** (key G, the "send a party" button): a paper map (roads, blocks, the school) with a pin for each known place (an icon by kind; unknown places are faint question marks near known ones), the selected place's card (name, blurb, danger, distance in hours, what is likely, nest, survivor, outpost), the party picker as in the old dialog (one to three people, who is free), the buttons Go, Scout, Clear, Claim, Garrison and Recall, and a line for each party out (where, due back). It replaces `scavenge_dialog.gd` in the settlement game.

**Harness:** `--crew=3` plays it; the policy chooses destinations by need and distance, scouts early, claims the farm, and rescues survivors.
