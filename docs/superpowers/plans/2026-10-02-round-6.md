## Design

**Plots** (`assets/data/plots.json`, owner VIEW for positions): twelve fixed lots in the compound, each with an id, a name, a `kind` (`yard` or `wall`), a centre `pos` and a `size` in tiles, and a `work` spot (where builders stand; walkable, outside the footprint, reachable). Nine yard plots (the court's two ends, the east and north-east lots, the south and south-east lots, the junk corner, beside the garden, the south-east corner) and three wall plots (north, west, east, against the decks). A plot holds one site or one building. Plots are drawn faintly in the yard (dashed outline, a small sign) and brighter when the Build screen is open or the mouse is over one.

**Buildings** (`assets/data/buildings.json`, owner Claude for the table, EFFECTS for effect numbers): each has `id`, `name`, `blurb` (one line of what it gives), `plots` (kinds it may stand on), `scrap` (paid when ordered), `work` (builder-hours at the base rate), `max` (how many), `requires` (a building that must stand), and `effects`, a flat map of numbers read through `sim.build.bonus(key)` (sum over standing buildings) and `sim.build.factor(key)` (product, 1.0 when none). The starting seven:

| Building | Plots | Scrap | Work | Effects (starting values) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Salvage bench | yard | 18 | 14 | unlocks the **Salvage** station (2 seats): each person at it sorts the yard's junk into 0.4 scrap an hour |
| Greenhouse beds | yard | 14 | 16 | `seats_garden` +2, `garden_base` +0.4 food at each dawn |
| Workshop | yard | 30 | 24 | `seats_bench` +1, `craft_mult` x1.25 (scrap an hour), `ammo_per_scrap` +1 |
| Bunk house | yard | 24 | 24 | `beds` 4 (patients rest in them), `housing` 4 (the cap round 7 uses), `morale` +3 (a bed to sleep in) |
| Floodlights | yard, wall | 28 | 20 | `night_reach_mult` x1.12 (the guns reach further by night), `blackout_proof` 1 (a blackout no longer costs aim) |
| Watchtower | wall | 36 | 32 | `reach_bonus` +3 tiles for people within `reach_radius` 4 tiles of the tower |
| Radio mast | yard | 30 | 28 | `boon_mult` x1.3 and `threat_mult` x0.85 on the radio cards, `radio_range` +1 (round 7) |

**Construction.** Ordering pays the scrap at once and puts a **site** on the plot. By day the people on the **Builders** job (station id `builders`, seats 3) who are free walk to the site of the oldest order and build it: progress per builder-hour is `1.0 + 0.12 x Building` times their performance and their `repair` trait, so a good builder is worth two; every 10 work-hours credit Building skill as 100 HP mended would. A wall under the crew's threshold still comes first (a person sent to mend a wall is on that order). At night the crew holds the posts and the site waits. A finished site becomes the building: its footprint (if any) turns solid in the walking grids, its effects start, the crew is told. **Cancel** a site (the scrap back in full if no work was done, else half); **Demolish** a building (half the scrap back, effects end, the footprint opens, a station that loses seats lets the extra people go).

**Stations from buildings.** The seats of a station are its base seats plus `seats_<station id>` from the standing buildings (`jobs.seats_of(id)`); `jobs.stations()` lists the stations that exist now (the Salvage station only once the bench stands). Everything in round 5 that read a station's `seats` reads `seats_of`.

**Round 7 hooks:** `sim.build.housing()` (the school's own 6 plus `housing`) and `sim.build.radio_range()`.

## Interfaces (the core in `scripts/sim/build.gd` carries them; built first, by Claude)

`sim.build` (module):
- `plots() -> Array` of `{"id","name","kind","pos": Vector2,"size": Vector2,"work": Vector2,"building": "" or id,"site": {} or {"building","progress","work","scrap"}}` (read-only for others); `plot(id)`; `plot_at(tile_pos: Vector2) -> String` (the plot whose lot contains the point, or "").
- `catalogue() -> Array` and `building(id) -> Dictionary` (the data, `scrap`, `work`, `plots`, `effects`, `blurb`, `name`, `max`, `requires`); `built(id) -> bool`; `count(id) -> int`; `bonus(key) -> float`; `factor(key) -> float`; `housing() -> int`; `radio_range() -> int`.
- `why_not(plot_id, building_id) -> String` ("" when it can be ordered now: the plot is empty, the kind fits, under `max` counting sites, the requirement stands, the scrap is there); `order(plot_id, building_id) -> bool`; `cancel(plot_id) -> bool`; `demolish(plot_id) -> bool`.
- `progress_of(plot_id) -> float` (0 to 1 of the site); `active_site() -> Dictionary` (the plot being worked, or {}); `crew() -> Array` (the builders at the site right now); `work_spot(p) -> Vector2` (where this builder stands at the active site).
- `footprint_tiles(plot_id) -> Array` of tile indices the standing building makes solid (from `assets/data/building_art.json`, `solid` rect relative to the plot centre; none when absent).
- events `{"type": "site"|"built"|"cancelled"|"demolished", "plot", "building"}`; alerts `built-<plot id>` (info, "The Bunk house is finished").
- `save()` / `load_state(d)`: plots' `building` and `site`; missing = nothing built.
`sim.jobs` gains `seats_of(id) -> int`; `stations()` lists the existing stations; the builders' day goal is the work spot of the active site while there is one (and the person is not on a repair order); the Salvage station (`stations.json`, `requires: "salvage_bench"`) produces its scrap in `build.update`.
`sim.block_tiles(tiles: Array, blocked: bool)` (in `sim.gd`): the walking grids and both path grids, as `open_segment` does for a wall.
