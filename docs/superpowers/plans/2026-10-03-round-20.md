## What the owner decided (and what this round does with it)

- The community **proposes ideas over the radio** ("I could fix the east wall properly for 20 scrap", "the garden could feed two more"); the player says yes or no. Each idea is a radio call (`kind "idea"`, options yes / later / no, nothing is random, nothing is a command) whose yes does something real through an EXISTING sim action (table S4.3). A no costs: the proposer's trust and mood, and a person refused three times in four days goes quiet for three days.
- The community **forms relationships** (friends, rivals, couples) and someone **emerges as the person others trust** (the voice: proposes more, and their opinion shows on the radio's calls). They feed round 17's rules: the bonds' morale and aim effects, `radio.willingness` (a friend in the squad warms a request, a hurt friend keeps someone home, an enemy in the squad refuses), the trust echo (push Dez and his friends mind), succession.
- The community **develops specialists** (the medic, the engineer, the marksman, the scout): earned by skill and practice, a title and a small bonus on exactly that work, first choice for the seat, the one whose opinion the call shows, the only one who makes some ideas; when they fall the gap shows.
- Left for LATER (not planned here): self-directed work, crises and change, growth; also feelings that are not mutual, a relationship matrix page, a patrol idea (no rule stands behind it).
- **Avatar mode only, in the settlement game** (`active()`, below). With no avatar every new hook returns the exact neutral value (`+ 0.0`, `* 1.0`, `[]`, `null`) and the game plays byte for byte as before: a test pins it (`tests/test_round20_noavatar.gd`).

## What stays (do not touch)

Round 17's radio (verbs, trust numbers, `raise_call`, the existing calls, alarm) and round 18's pages and interact module, except the hook lines of S4.4; the storyteller's cards (`argument` and `old_friends` do not feed relations this round); `skills.gd`'s growth rules; `bonds.gd`'s roster list and `save()` (relation friends are merged at query time, never stored there); no new `Person` variable (all state is in the three modules); no use of `sim.rng` anywhere in them (every choice is a pure function of the state, ties by id), so a saved game replays bit for bit.

### S4.1 RELATIONS (`scripts/sim/relations.gd`, `assets/data/chemistry.json`)

Pairwise **affinity**, -100 to 100, symmetric (deliberately: one number a pair), stored per pair `"a|b"` (ids sorted) as `{"v", "kind", "since" (day the kind began), "why", "day"}`, with an adjacency index `_adj[id]` kept on every write (queries are O(friends): the panels read willingness at 10 Hz for everybody). A pair with the current player is never moved (trust is the player's own number); its stored value stays. Someone who is not in `sim.people` has no pairs (the small start removes the late callers after `start()`): each look drops such pairs, and `person_added` seeds the newcomer's roster pairs from `sim.bonds.list` at their anchors (so `start()` and `person_added` share one `_seed_roster()`).

```

const RIVAL_AT := -35.0; RIVAL_END := -20.0; ENEMY_AT := -65.0          # kinds have hysteresis (begin at X, end under Y); each person: at most 3 friends, 1 partner, 2 rivals (the strongest keep the kind)
const ANCHOR := {"friend": 55.0, "family": 60.0, "mentor": 50.0}; ANCHOR_FLOOR := 40.0   # a roster bond (sim.bonds.list) starts there and never drifts under the floor; only an event can break it
const STATION_GAIN := 0.25; NEAR_GAIN := 0.15; POST_GAIN := 0.2; MEAL_GAIN := 0.8; DECAY_PER_DAY := 1.0   # per slow look; gains x (1 - |v|/120); decay toward 0 once a day
const TRIP_GAIN := 2.5; TRIP_HURT_GAIN := 4.0; RESCUE_GAIN := 6.0; RESCUE_DOWNED_GAIN := 10.0; SCARCE_LOSS := -0.5; TOGETHER_TILES := 2.6; POST_NEIGHBOUR_TILES := 5.2

const VOICE_MIN := 40.0; VOICE_SWITCH := 8.0; COMMUNITY_MIN := 35.0; NEWS_PER_DAY := 2

func add(a, b, delta: float, why: String) -> void   # THE writer (initiative uses it too): clamps, remembers `why`, re-derives kinds with the caps, logs news when a kind begins; no-op inactive, self, or the player
func friends_of(p) -> Array; func rivals_of(p) -> Array; func partner_of(p)   # alive people at the base or away, strongest first; partner_of: Person or null
func circle(p) -> Array                 # [{"who","kind","role"}] friends and the partner who are NOT a pair of `sim.bonds.list` for p (role "friend" / "partner"; it never calls `bonds.of`, which calls it); [] when inactive
func is_close(p, q) -> bool             # a friend or partner pair (for bonds.bonded); false inactive
func describe(p) -> Dictionary          # for the views: {"friends": [{"id","first","kind","word","value"}], "rivals": [...], "partner": {} or one, "lines": ["Close to Ana, Marcus (family)", "At odds with Walt"]}; empty inactive

func voice()                            # Person or null: the best standing >= VOICE_MIN among the living at the base who are not the player; sticky (changes only when beaten by VOICE_SWITCH); news "Ana is who people turn to now"
func community_trust() -> float         # the leader trust: the mean Person.trust of the living who are not the player (55 at the start)
func company_pull(contact, verb: String) -> float                # radio.willingness adds it to the trust it reads (never stored): +6 for come/cover/search when a friend or partner of `contact` already follows or covers the leader (or has been agreed to), -6 for a rival; 0.0 otherwise or inactive
func company_reason(contact, what: String, station_id := "", with_ids := []) -> String   # a SOFT reason in the radio's words, "" when none; what: "come" | "cover" | "search" | "go" | "work"
        # go-like: "staying with Dez" (a friend or partner at the base is down, bleeding, in a bed or has a fever and nobody else is with them), "won't go with Walt" (an ENEMY is in the squad / in `with_ids`)
        # work: "won't work beside Walt" (an enemy holds a seat at `station_id`). Insisting overrides it at the usual cost.
func rival_near(p)                      # Person or null (morale: "On edge near Walt", RIVAL_NEAR); func grief_mult(p, lost) -> float  # 1.3 when `lost` was p's partner, else 1.0

func succession_bonus(p) -> float       # SUCCESSION_SHARE x standing(p): avatar._before compares `trust + succession_bonus`
func on_trust_moved(p, delta: float) -> void   # radio.add_trust calls it: a push or a gain of ECHO_MIN or more reaches p's friends at ECHO_SHARE with the reason "How you treated Dez" (once an hour a pair; the echo itself never echoes)
func on_treated(healer, patient, kind: String) -> void   # medical._finish: +RESCUE_GAIN (+RESCUE_DOWNED_GAIN if the patient was down)
func on_trip(came: Array, lost: Array, party: Dictionary) -> void   # scavenge._come_back: every pair that came home +TRIP_GAIN (+TRIP_HURT_GAIN with someone hurt)

```
Moves (on the slow look, only people up and at the base; 20 people = 190 pairs, a few hundred distance checks every 30 game minutes): **working together** STATION_GAIN for each pair in `jobs.working(station)`; **sharing a place** NEAR_GAIN for a pair within TOGETHER_TILES and still (the rest spots, a meal); **sharing a wall** POST_GAIN at night for posts within POST_NEIGHBOUR_TILES; **mealtimes** MEAL_GAIN for pairs within TOGETHER_TILES when the look crosses a `supplies.MEAL_HOURS` hour; **chemistry** (`chemistry.json`) scales a pair's gain (`warm`) or adds a friction or warmth term per look; **scarcity** SCARCE_LOSS per look for a pair together while either is hungry or thirsty (hunger frays tempers); **decay** once a day. Events are in the signatures above. News (radio `log_news`, at most NEWS_PER_DAY a day): kind "bond" "Dez and Ana are close now" / "Ana and Walt are together now"; kind "rift" "Walt and Dez are at odds" (sub: where they work or stand side by side); the voice line is kind "bond". The News is the only list of changes: no popups.

### S4.2 SPECIALISTS (`scripts/sim/specialists.gd`, `assets/data/titles.json`)

A title is held by one person at a time. **Eligible** when the skill is at least `min_skill` (6) and the practice is at least `need`; **score** = skill + min(2.0, practice / need); the best score takes a vacant title at once; a holder is displaced only by a score at least `SWITCH_MARGIN` (1.0) higher (no flip-flop). Evaluated at every slow look and at the dawn. Practice comes from `Person.stats` (medic: `treated`; engineer: `mended / 10 + crafted`; marksman: `kills`) and, for the scout, from the module's own `record[id].trips`.

```
const TITLES := {   # in titles.json: name, short, skill, min_skill, need, effects, seats, blurb
  "medic":    {"name": "the medic",    "skill": "Medicine", "need": 6.0,  "seat": "infirmary", "traits": {"bandage": 1.25}, "adds": {"cure": 0.08}},
  "engineer": {"name": "the engineer", "skill": "Building", "need": 30.0, "seat": "bench",     "traits": {"craft": 1.15, "repair": 1.2}},
  "marksman": {"name": "the marksman", "skill": "Shooting", "need": 20.0, "seat": "watch",     "traits": {"aim": 1.04}},
  "scout":    {"name": "the scout",    "skill": "",         "need": 3.0,  "seat": "",          "party_loot": 1.12}}   # scout: trips >= 3 and more than anyone else; no skill floor, no seat
func holder(title_id: String)                       # Person or null (alive, not left); "the one to call"
func title_of(p) -> Dictionary                      # {"id","name","short","since_day"} or {}; used by every view
func gaps() -> Array                                # [{"id","name","since_day","was": id}] titles lost in the last 6 days with no heir yet
func mult(p, key: String) -> float / add(p, key: String) -> float   # traits.mult / traits.add multiply and sum these in (1.0 / 0.0 when inactive or no title): ONE edit in traits.gd gives every site its bonus
        # (workshop.scrap_per_hour, build.rate, sim mending, medical bandage and cure_chance, sim.aim_factor), and so "on that work" is automatic: the key is read only while doing it
func fit_bonus(p, station_id: String) -> float      # jobs._fit: +1.5 for the title's own seat (Auto-assign and fill_empty put the medic in the infirmary), 0.0 otherwise
func party_mult(people: Array) -> float             # scavenge.loot_scale: 1.12 with the scout in the party, else 1.0
func on_trip(came: Array, lost: Array, party: Dictionary) -> void    # records one trip for each of `came` (dice errands and led trips alike)
func record_of(p) -> Dictionary                     # {"trips", "since": {title: day}}
func opinion_title(p) -> String                     # "the medic" or ""
```
**Becoming** logs News kind "title" "Ana is the camp's medic now" (sub: "Medicine 10, 8 treated"), with an alert for a minute, and a bark. **Losing** (the holder dies, turns, leaves, or is away more than 3 days): News "Ana was the medic. The camp has no medic now.", a shock to everyone at the base (`morale.shock(null, -3.0, "No medic: Ana is gone")`), the bonus goes, and `gaps()` lists it until an heir is named or 6 days pass. The first evaluation after a load of a save with no module data names titles silently (no "became" news).

## The views (V4.1 COMMUNITY-VIEW: `scripts/ui/community_kit.gd` new; small Edits in `hud.gd`, `radio_kit.gd`, `call_view.gd`)

Every read goes through `_mod("relations" | "specialists" | "initiative")` with `has_method` guards (an older fake has none: no row, no error). The cheapest solid view, no new screen:

- **News** (the radio's log, nothing new to build): kinds in `RadioKit`: "idea" (Idea, `host.INFO`), "bond" (People, `host.OK`), "rift" (Falling out, `host.WARN`), "title" (Known for, `host.ACCENT`). ## Risks
