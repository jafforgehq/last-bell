# Round 0: truth and foundation

> Follows the full audit ([docs/audit/2026-10-01-full-audit.md](../../audit/2026-10-01-full-audit.md)). The user said go on 2026-10-01 and approved: the dead can break into the school; the schoolyard-noticeboard UI direction (for the HUD and menus only: the user likes the world as it is now, with its fences, tiles and props, and said to keep it; the concept boards are references, not replacements); the game gets a new name (working title **Thirty Bells**, chosen by Claude on the user's delegation; one commit to swap).

**Goal:** make the numbers mean what they say, so that every later round is built on rules that bind: ammo gates shooting, defending beats hiding, the run has an end, the loop is deterministic, and the first sources of friction in the UI are gone.

**Branch:** `feature/colony-sandbox`. Nothing is pushed, merged to `main` or published.

## Global constraints

- Godot 4.7.2, GDScript. The suite (`--script res://tests/run.gd`) must stay green; any logged error fails a test. New behaviour gets a test that failed first.
- The rules stay deterministic: one seeded `sim.rng`, no global `randf`/`randi` in `scripts/sim`.
- A save from before this round may be refused (VERSION bump is allowed), but a save written by this round must load back to the same game.
- Do not publish, push or touch `main`. Deletion goes through the Trash.

## Acceptance

- **T1:** with 0 ammo a night's kills are far fewer than with ammo (the opposite of the audit's 32 and 32); scavenged ammo makes the guns fire again; the HUD says why they stopped.
- **T2:** with `--policy=keep` the colony does not outlive the default policy (the audit: 6 of 6 alive on day 20 in 3 of 3 seeds); with the default policy the median day does not fall by more than about 2 days from 17 without a deliberate retune.
- **T3:** `sim.over == "victory"` exactly once, at the dawn that starts day 31, with at least one survivor; the harness counts it as survived.
- **T4:** the same seed gives the same game at 30, 60 and 144 fps and with uneven frames (a test drives the clock with different frame times and compares fingerprints); drawing is smooth between steps.
- **T5:** `Pathfinder.smooth` does a bounded number of sight checks per path point; short paths come out identical to before.
- **T6:** every order button has a tooltip; no UI text under 12 px; secondary text contrast at least 4.5:1; a changing wall alert does not rebuild the feed; no orphan controls; the help panel lists every key.
- **All:** the full suite is green; the harness runs; screenshots of the changed UI and the school doors look right.
