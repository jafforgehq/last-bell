You are Ruth. It is seven in the morning, the bell is ringing somewhere, and you are alone at the edge of a dead town with a pump shotgun and a few shells.

Find a house. Clear every room, search it, and claim it. Then talk to the voices on the radio: the people who answer will come and help, if you put down the dead between you and them. When two of them are home, the dead start to come every night, and they come to the bell. Fence the house, hang floodlights, build a watchtower, and hold until dawn.

This is a demo (about 20 to 30 minutes). It covers the first day or two and your first attack night. The town is generated for each run: a different town for every seed, and the same number gives the same town again.

What you do

  • Clear a house room by room, search it, and claim it as your base.
  • Search the street too: parked cars hold loot (a few have nothing), and your people search them on their own when you send them out.
  • Carry what you find in a grid backpack (Tab or I): searching a container reveals its items one by one, you take what fits, and you deposit at the stash in your base so the crew can use it. Your shells for the shotgun come from the pack.
  • Rescue survivors over the radio. They take jobs (builders, lookouts, scavengers, the radio desk) and answer when you ask. At night the crew patrols the fence by itself and shoots what comes; anyone you put in a watchtower stays there.
  • Build the base: front fence, gate, watchtowers, side and back fences, floodlights, planks on the doors. You can build the first fence with your own hands.
  • Hold the first night. It includes a plated brute: aim for its head. There is a day and a night, and rain, storms and lightning are part of the weather. They can fall on the night itself.

Controls

  • W A S D move, X run, right mouse aim, left mouse fire, R reload, F swing the gun's butt
  • E use (search, claim, build, talk, sleep, treat), Q put the weapon away
  • T radio, M map, N build page, . (full stop) wait for the bell, Tab or I backpack, H hide or show the HUD, Esc menu
  • Hold E in a cleared room to search all of it. Press E at a fence site and stay by it to build it yourself.

Good to know

  • The game needs a keyboard and a mouse. It was tested in Chrome on a desktop; other recent desktop browsers should work. For full screen use the button under the game.
  • The Settings page has a Brightness slider (70 to 130 percent), a HUD setting (Minimal is the default: the screen starts nearly clear and shows more as it matters; Full shows everything), switches for reduced motion and reduced flashes, colour-blind markers and text size.
  • The first load downloads about 80 MB. After that it starts in a few seconds.
  • Saves are kept in your browser. A save from an older version of the game cannot be continued; the game says so and you start a new one.
